# 42. Online Shopping

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search for** | To look online for specific products or items | Шукати |
| **Filter the results** | To narrow down search results by category, size, color, brand, or price | Відфільтрувати результати |
| **Sort by price / popularity** | To arrange products in order of cost or customer rating | Сортувати за ціною / популярністю |
| **Select your size** | To choose the correct dimensions or size before purchasing clothes or shoes | Обрати свій розмір |
| **Check delivery options** | To review available shipping methods and estimated arrival times | Перевірити варіанти доставки |
| **Add to cart / basket** | To save an item in your digital shopping cart before finalizing a purchase | Додати в кошик |
| **Choose your payment method** | To select how to pay for an order (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or cash on delivery) | Обрати спосіб оплати |
| **Confirm your order** | To verify and accept your purchase details before final payment | Підтвердити замовлення |
| **Track your order** | To follow the real-time shipping and delivery progress of a package online | Відстежувати замовлення |
| **Get free shipping** | To receive parcel delivery without paying extra transportation fees | Отримати безкоштовну доставку |
| **Delay in delivery** | A situation where a parcel arrives later than the scheduled or expected time | Затримка доставки |
| **Package was damaged** | A condition where a parcel or box is broken, crushed, or opened during transit | Пакування було пошкоджене |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Always **filter the results** and **check delivery options** before you **add to cart**, so you never have to worry about a **delay in delivery**!"

> "When shopping online, **sort by price** to find the best deal, **confirm your order**, and **track your order** until it arrives safely."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Prepositional Combinations): `Verb + Preposition + Noun`
Formula (First Conditional for Shopping): `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + can / will + Verb (Infinitive)`

* **Прийменники та фразові сполучення в онлайн-шопінгу (Prepositions with Shopping Verbs):**
  У контексті онлайн-покупок дієслова вимагають вживання чітких прийменників:
  * **Search for** — шукати щось конкретне (*search for shoes*).
  * **Filter by / Sort by** — фільтрувати або сортувати за певним критерієм (*sort by price, filter by size*).
  * **Delay in** — затримка у чомусь (*delay in delivery*).

* **Перший умовний спосіб для умов доставки та покупок (First Conditional):**
  Використовується для опису реальних умов отримання знижок, безкоштовної доставки або наслідків пошкодження товару у майбутньому.

*Example: I spent ten minutes searching for the right shoes on the website.*
*Example: You can filter the results by color and sort them by price.*
*Example: If you spend more than \$50, you will get free shipping.*
*Example: There was a delay in delivery because the warehouse was very busy.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What’s your favourite online shop? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say my favourite online store is Amazon because it allows you to filter the results easily and always offers reliable delivery options.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I don't have one specific favourite shop; I usually search for items across multiple platforms and sort by price to find the best deal.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really enjoy shopping online, as I find browsing physical stores much more dependable when selecting sizes.

**Q: How do you usually browse and select products when shopping online?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always filter the results by size and color first, and then sort by popularity to see customer ratings before making a decision.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I simply type what I need in the search bar and compare two or three options without applying too many filters.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I spend far too much time comparing different items, which often leads to putting things in my cart without actually buying them.

**Q: What do you do if there is a delay in delivery or your package arrives damaged?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if there is a delay in delivery, I immediately track my order online and send a polite message to customer support to resolve the issue.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually wait a few extra days if there is a delay, but if the package arrives damaged, I immediately request a refund.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find dealing with damaged packages extremely frustrating, so I try to avoid buying fragile items online altogether.

**Q: Do you prefer paying by credit card, PayPal, or upon delivery?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I prefer to choose PayPal or Apple Pay as my payment method because it is fast, secure, and protects my purchase details.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually pay with a credit card to get cashback, though paying upon delivery is also a reasonable option.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I feel uncomfortable entering my card details on unfamiliar websites, so I only order from verified platforms.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Resolving a Logistics & Shipping Issue**
* **Context:** An e-commerce customer support representative addressing a corporate client's delayed shipment.
* **Role A:** "Good morning. Could you please check the status of order #4082? We experienced a **delay in delivery** for our office equipment."
* **Role B:** "Actually, let me **track your order** in our system right now. It appears there was a brief delay at the regional warehouse."
* **Role A:** "I see. Will we still receive **free shipping** as agreed in our initial contract?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes, absolutely. Furthermore, if the **package was damaged** in transit, we will dispatch a replacement immediately."

**Scenario 2: Optimizing E-Commerce Platform User Experience**
* **Context:** A product designer and a marketing manager discussing features for an online store.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss how to improve our checkout conversion rate for the new clothing line?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest allowing users to **filter the results** by size and **sort by price** more intuitively on mobile screens."
* **Role A:** "That makes sense. Should we also simplify the step where customers **choose their payment method**?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. Once they **add to cart** and **confirm their order**, the payment process should take no more than two clicks."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive online shopping experiences, suggest helpful website features, or clarify order details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing candid thoughts about delivery delays, payment preferences, or online shopping habits gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion complaints about damaged packages, shipping delays, or missing stock gently. |
| **Could you please check...** | An indirect, respectful way to request customer support assistance regarding order tracking or refunds. |
| **I'd suggest that we...** | Used in professional e-commerce contexts to propose website user interface improvements or shipping policies. |
| **Would it be possible to...** | A polite, formal construction for requesting order modifications, address changes, or expedited delivery. |
