# 22. Banking and Finances

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Open an account** | To start using a new bank account to store or manage money | Відкрити рахунок у банку |
| **Check your account balance** | To review the exact amount of money available in your bank account | Перевірити баланс рахунку |
| **Pay bills** | To settle payments for services such as utilities, internet, or loans | Сплачувати рахунки |
| **Take out money** | To withdraw cash from an automated teller machine (ATM) or bank branch | Знімати гроші (з банкомата) |
| **Apply for a credit card** | To submit a formal request to a bank for a credit card facility | Подати заявку на кредитну картку |
| **Max out a credit card** | To spend up to the maximum allowable credit limit on a card | Витратити весь ліміт кредитної картки |
| **Set up automatic payments** | To arrange scheduled, automated electronic transfers for recurring bills | Налаштувати автоматичні платежі |
| **Transfer money to a personal account** | To move funds electronically from one bank account to another | Переказувати гроші на особистий рахунок |
| **Request a bank statement** | To ask the bank for an official document summarizing account transactions | Запросити виписку з банківського рахунку |
| **In the red** | Having a negative account balance or owing money to the bank | Мати негативний баланс (бути «в мінусі») |
| **In the black** | Having a positive account balance with no active overdraft | Мати позитивний баланс (бути «в плюсі») |
| **Interest rate** | The percentage charged on borrowed money or paid on savings | Відсоткова ставка |
| **Mortgage** | A specialized long-term bank loan used specifically for purchasing real estate | Іпотека |
| **Pay in installments** | To pay for an item in smaller, regular amounts over a specified period | Платити частинами / на виплат |
| **Pay off a loan** | To completely repay all principal and interest owed on a borrowed sum | Виплатити позику / кредит повністю |
| **Be in debt** | To owe money to individuals, financial institutions, or creditors | Бути в боргах / мати борг |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "To keep your account **in the black**, always **check your account balance** regularly before you **max out your credit card**!"

> "Taking out a **mortgage** allows you to buy property and **pay in installments** until you **pay off the loan** completely!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Giving Financial Advice): `Subject + should / need to + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Unreal Conditional Advice): `If I were you, I would + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Financial Status Expressions): `Subject + be + in the red / in the black / in debt`  

* **Надання порад щодо фінансового планування (Giving Advice):**  
  Для висловлення порад щодо управління грошима вживаються модальні дієслова *should*, *need to* та умовна конструкція *If I were you, I would...* (*You should set up automatic payments for monthly utilities*).
* **Вираження фінансового стану (Financial Status Expressions):**  
  Ідіоми *in the red* (від'ємний баланс) та *in the black* (позитивний баланс) описують поточний стан рахунку. Конструкція *be in debt* вказує на наявність боргових зобов'язань.
* **Прийменники та фразові дієслова у банківській сфері (Banking Phrasal Verbs & Prepositions):**  
  * **take out** money (*знімати гроші з рахунку чи банкомата*).  
  * **set up** automatic payments (*налаштовувати автоплатежі*).  
  * **pay off** a loan (*повністю погашати кредит*).  
  * **pay in** installments (*сплачувати рівними частинами*).  

*Example: You should check your account balance regularly to avoid overdraft fees.*  
*Example: If I were you, I would set up automatic payments so I never miss a bill deadline.*  
*Example: After getting my salary, my bank account is finally in the black again.*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Choose the Correct Definition (Multiple Choice)

1. I went to the bank yesterday to **open an account** because I want to keep my savings safe.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **Start using a new bank account**
2. This bank offers a low **interest rate**, so it’s a good place to borrow money.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **The percentage you pay for borrowing money**
3. They took out a **mortgage** to buy their new house.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **A loan specifically for buying property**
4. I can’t pay the full amount now, so I will **pay in installments**.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **Pay in smaller parts over time**
5. It took him five years to **pay off a loan** completely.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **Finish paying all borrowed money**
6. After buying a car, she is now **in debt**.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **Owes money**
7. I spent too much this month and now I’m **in the red**.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **My balance is negative**
8. After getting my salary, I’m finally **in the black** again.  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) **With a positive balance**

### Exercise 2: Fill in the Gaps (Bank Operations)

1. She wants to **open an account** for her daughter at the bank.
2. You should **check your account balance** regularly to avoid overdraft fees.
3. I usually **pay my bills** online at the end of the month.
4. I need to **take out money** from the ATM before the shops close.
5. He decided to **apply for a credit card** to build his credit history.

### Exercise 3: Fill in the Gaps (Financial Management)

1. Try not to **max out your credit card** on things you don’t really need.
2. I **set up automatic payments** so I never forget to pay my phone bill.
3. My company will **transfer money to a personal account** every month as my salary.
4. If you need proof of income, you can **request a bank statement** from your bank.
5. After buying that car, his account was **in the red** for months.

### Translation Block

* Вона хоче відкрити рахунок для своєї доньки у банку. — *She wants to **open an account** for her daughter at the bank.*
* Всі повинні регулярно перевіряти баланс рахунку, щоб уникнути комісій. — *You should **check your account balance** regularly to avoid overdraft fees.*
* Налаштуйте автоматичні платежі, щоб ніколи не запізнюватися зі сплатою рахунків. — *Set up **automatic payments** so you never miss a bill deadline.*
* Вони взяли іпотеку, щоб купити новий будинок. — *They took out a **mortgage** to buy their new house.*
* Після отримання зарплати мій баланс знову позитивний («в плюсі»). — *After getting my salary, I'm finally **in the black** again.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you prefer paying for expensive items in full or paying in installments?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'd say paying in full is always better because you avoid paying extra interest rates and stay completely out of debt.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the item; for buying real estate, taking out a mortgage and paying in installments is practical, but for consumer gadgets, I pay upfront.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't like paying in installments, as having recurring monthly debts creates unnecessary financial anxiety.

**Q: How often do you check your account balance or request a bank statement using online banking?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I check my account balance every morning via my mobile app to manage my weekly budget and ensure my balance stays in the black.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I check it a couple of times a month before paying utility bills or transferring funds.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely request bank statements unless I suspect an unexpected banking fee, to be honest.

**Q: What advice would you give to someone who keeps maxing out their credit card and stays in the red?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if I were you, I would set up a strict monthly budget and cut back on non-essential spending immediately.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, they should focus on paying off high-interest loans first and avoid using credit cards for daily purchases.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid managing chronic credit card debt requires drastic lifestyle changes, so seeking professional financial advice might be necessary.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Opening a Corporate Account & Financial Services**
* **Context:** A business owner discussing financial options with a bank representative.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! I'd like to **open an account** for my new corporate consulting firm today."
* **Role B:** "Actually, welcome! We can process your paperwork right away. Would you also like to **apply for a credit card** for company expenses?"
* **Role A:** "To be honest, yes, but what is the annual **interest rate** on corporate cards?"
* **Role B:** "We offer a competitive rate, and you can easily **set up automatic payments** through our mobile banking app."

**Scenario 2: Discussing Mortgage & Loan Repayment Options**
* **Context:** A client consulting a loan officer regarding property financing.
* **Role A:** "We are planning to take out a **mortgage** to purchase a commercial property."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, that's a major step. Our bank provides flexible terms where you can **pay in installments** over fifteen years."
* **Role A:** "Could we **pay off the loan** earlier without incurring additional penalty fees?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! Early repayment helps you reduce total interest charges and ensures your business stays **in the black**."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive financial habits, clarify account terms, or confirm payment details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly discussing debt, admitting budgeting mistakes, or giving realistic financial advice. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion statements about negative balances, declined loan applications, or high interest rates gently. |
| **If I were you, I would...** | A polite, non-intrusive formula for offering financial advice and budgeting tips to friends or clients. |
| **It allows you to...** | Used when explaining the practical benefits of mobile banking tools, such as setting up automatic payments. |
