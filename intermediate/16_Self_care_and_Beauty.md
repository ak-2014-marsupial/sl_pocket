# 16. Self-care and Beauty Globally

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Moisturize your face and body** | To apply cream or lotion to make the skin soft and prevent dryness | Зволожити обличчя та тіло |
| **Try a face mask / sheet mask** | To apply a cosmetic or fabric mask to nourish or hydrate facial skin | Спробувати маску / тканинну маску |
| **Trim your beard** | To cut facial hair slightly using clippers or scissors to keep it neat | Підстригти бороду |
| **Dye your hair** | To change the color of one's hair using cosmetic dye | Пофарбувати волосся |
| **Pluck your eyebrows** | To pull out individual eyebrow hairs with tweezers to shape them | Вищипати брови |
| **Get a manicure / pedicure** | To undergo a professional cosmetic care treatment for hands/nails or feet | Зробити манікюр / педикюр |
| **Whiten your teeth** | To make teeth lighter and brighter using dental or at-home treatments | Відбілити зуби |
| **Try Botox** | To get cosmetic injections to temporarily reduce or prevent facial wrinkles | Спробувати ботокс |
| **Get lip / jawline fillers** | To receive cosmetic injections to add volume or contouring to lips or jawline | Зробити філери для губ / лінії щелепи |
| **Try laser hair removal** | To undergo a light-based procedure for long-lasting hair removal | Спробувати лазерну епіляцію |
| **Get eyelash extensions** | To attach synthetic lashes to natural ones for added volume and length | Зробити нарощення вій |
| **Get a nose job** | To undergo rhinoplasty surgery to reshape the nose or fix breathing issues | Зробити ринопластику (операцію на ніс) |
| **Try a facelift** | To undergo a cosmetic procedure to tighten facial skin and reduce sagging | Спробувати підтяжку обличчя |
| **Get a hair transplant** | To undergo a medical procedure to restore hair in balding areas | Зробити пересадку волосся |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Always **moisturize your face and body** after taking a shower, and treat yourself to **try a face mask** on weekends!"

> "Whether you decide to **get eyelash extensions** or **whiten your teeth**, do what makes you feel confident and comfortable!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Recommendations): `Subject + should / can / could + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Condition & Advice): `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + should / can + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Imperative Advice): `Don't / Be careful not to + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  

* **Надання порад щодо догляду за собою (Giving Self-care Recommendations):**  
  Дієслово **should** вживається для надання порад та рекомендацій щодо краси та догляду за здоров'ям.  
  * *If you have dry skin, you should moisturize your body more often.*  
  * *You should pluck your eyebrows carefully to shape them.*

* **Дієслівні сполучення (Collocations for Beauty Treatments):**  
  З естетичними процедурами вживаються конкретні дієслова:  
  * **get:** *get a manicure / pedicure, get lip fillers, get a nose job, get a hair transplant, get eyelash extensions*.  
  * **try:** *try a face mask, try Botox, try laser hair removal, try a facelift*.  
  * **do / action verb:** *pluck eyebrows, trim beard, dye hair, whiten teeth, moisturize skin*.  

*Example: He trims his beard once a week to keep it neat.*  
*Example: I got eyelash extensions before the holiday so I wouldn't need mascara.*  
*Example: Many people whiten their teeth before special occasions to look their best.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Tell us about your daily or weekly self-care routine. What procedures do you do at home?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I make sure to moisturize my face and body every morning and try a sheet mask every Sunday to relax completely.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I keep my routine simple — I trim my beard once a week and use basic skincare products.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't spend much time on complex routines, to be honest, though I always moisturize dry skin when needed.

**Q: If you could choose one cosmetic treatment for free, would it be lip fillers, jawline fillers, or laser hair removal?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'd say I would choose laser hair removal because it offers long-lasting results and saves a lot of time.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on individual needs; I think whitening your teeth or getting eyelash extensions provides great subtle changes.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I'm quite hesitant about aesthetic procedures like fillers or Botox, to be honest, as I prefer natural skincare.

**Q: Do you think invasive procedures like a nose job or hair transplant are becoming more accepted socially?**
* **Positive:** Actually, yes; many people get a nose job to fix breathing issues or get a hair transplant to regain confidence, and society is much more supportive now.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, people view surgical changes as a personal choice nowadays, whether someone tries a facelift or minor cosmetic adjustments.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid some cosmetic surgeries carry unnecessary risks, so I always recommend non-invasive self-care first.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discussing Beauty Services & Client Scheduling**
* **Context:** An administrator at a beauty salon consulting a client regarding pre-holiday appointments.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! Are you looking to schedule your pre-holiday aesthetic appointments today?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! I'd like to **get a manicure** and **get eyelash extensions** before next weekend."
* **Role A:** "Certainly. Would you also like to book a slot to **dye your hair** or **try a face mask** treatment?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I already **dyed my hair** last week, but I'd love to add a facial moisturizing session if available."

**Scenario 2: Aesthetic Clinic Consultation**
* **Context:** A cosmetologist discussing non-surgical facial refresh options with a patient.
* **Role A:** "Welcome to our clinic! What specific skin or facial concerns would you like to address today?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I've noticed fine lines on my forehead, so I was wondering if I should **try Botox** or **get lip fillers**."
* **Role A:** "Actually, if you want to reduce forehead wrinkles, **trying Botox** is very effective, whereas fillers add natural volume."
* **Role B:** "That makes sense! I'll start with a light treatment and make sure to **moisturize my face** regularly at home."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share personal beauty preferences, confirm appointment choices, or offer positive recommendations politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when admitting hesitation about cosmetic procedures, sharing candid opinions, or giving neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion statements when declining invasive cosmetic treatments or expressing safety concerns gently. |
| **You should try to...** | A polite, non-intrusive way to suggest skincare tips or non-invasive treatments to friends or clients. |
| **It gives long-lasting results** | A persuasive, professional phrase used when explaining the benefits of procedures like laser hair removal. |
