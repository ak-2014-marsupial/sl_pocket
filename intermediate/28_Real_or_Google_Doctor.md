# 28. Real or 'Google' Doctor?

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pollen allergy** | An allergic reaction triggered by plant pollen causing sneezing, watery eyes, and congestion | Алергія на пилок |
| **Asthma** | A chronic respiratory condition characterized by difficulty breathing and airway inflammation | Астма |
| **Insomnia** | A sleep disorder characterized by persistent difficulty falling or staying asleep | Безсоння |
| **Acne** | A skin condition involving inflamed or infected sebaceous glands causing pimples | Акне / вугровий висип |
| **Migraine** | A recurrent, severe throbbing headache often accompanied by nausea and sensitivity to light | Мігрень |
| **Diabetes** | A metabolic disease causing high blood sugar levels due to inadequate insulin production or response | Цукровий діабет |
| **High blood pressure** | A condition where the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high | Високий кров'яний тиск |
| **Take antihistamine** | To consume anti-allergy medication that blocks histamine response | Приймати антигістамінні препарати |
| **Use an inhaler** | To breathe in aerosolized medication through a medical device to relieve breathing distress | Використовувати інгалятор |
| **Monitor blood sugar level** | To regularly test and track glucose levels in the bloodstream | Відстежувати рівень цукру в крові |
| **Use acne gels** | To apply topical dermatological treatments to reduce facial skin inflammation and blemishes | Використовувати гелі від акне |
| **Reduce salt intake** | To decrease dietary sodium consumption to help lower blood pressure | Зменшити споживання солі |
| **Rest in a dark, quiet room** | To lie down in an environment free of bright light and noise to soothe severe headaches | Відпочивати у темній, тихій кімнаті |
| **Take insulin** | To administer synthetic insulin hormone to regulate glucose in diabetic patients | Приймати / колоти інсулін |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "If you have a **pollen allergy**, always **take antihistamine** before going outside in spring!"

> "When suffering from a severe **migraine**, it is best to **rest in a dark, quiet room** until the pain passes!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Giving Advice): `Subject + should + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Expressing Necessity): `Subject + need to + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  
Formula (Recommending Actions): `It's a good idea to + Verb (Bare Infinitive)`  

* **Конструкції вираження порад та рекомендацій щодо здоров'я (Giving Health Advice):**  
  Дієслова **should**, **need to** та фраза **It's a good idea to...** використовуються для надання медичних рекомендацій та порад щодо покращення самопочуття при різних симптомах чи захворюваннях.  
  * **You should...** — вживається для надання дружньої або професійної поради (*You should reduce salt intake*).  
  * **You need to...** — вживається, коли дію необхідно виконати обов'язково для здоров'я (*You need to monitor blood sugar level*).  
  * **It's a good idea to...** — пом'якшена форма поради (*It's a good idea to use an inhaler*).

*Example: If you have high blood pressure, you should reduce salt intake immediately.*  
*Example: People with diabetes need to monitor blood sugar levels regularly.*  
*Example: When you feel a migraine coming on, it's a good idea to rest in a dark, quiet room.*

### Translation Block

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you trust Google for health advice or real doctors more? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always consult real doctors because professional medical diagnosis is far more reliable than searching symptoms online.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the situation; I might look up general wellness tips on Google, but I visit a doctor for prescriptions or serious symptoms.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid searching symptoms online often causes unnecessary health anxiety, so I try to avoid 'Google doctoring' altogether.

**Q: Do you sleep well at night or do you sometimes suffer from insomnia?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I maintain a consistent sleep schedule, so I rarely experience insomnia or fatigue during the day.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally struggle with insomnia when I am under high stress or working late on projects.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid insomnia affects my concentration quite often, to be honest, especially when I drink coffee late in the evening.

**Q: How often do you check your blood pressure or blood sugar levels?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I check my blood pressure regularly as part of my general preventative health routine.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I only check my blood pressure during annual health check-ups or when I feel a headache coming on.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't check my blood sugar or pressure at home, as I haven't experienced any chronic symptoms so far.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discussing Health Symptoms at a Clinic**
* **Context:** A patient consulting a family doctor regarding seasonal allergy symptoms.
* **Role A:** "Good morning, doctor! Every spring when trees bloom, I start sneezing and my eyes get itchy."
* **Role B:** "Actually, it sounds like you have a **pollen allergy**. You should **take antihistamine** daily during the flowering season."
* **Role A:** "Thank you! Should I also avoid outdoor activities when the pollen count is high?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. It's a good idea to limit outdoor exercise on windy days to prevent symptom flare-ups."

**Scenario 2: Managing Workplace Wellness & Stress**
* **Context:** A corporate wellness coordinator discussing health management with an employee experiencing frequent headaches.
* **Role A:** "Hi Alex, you looked quite exhausted during the morning strategy sync. Is everything alright?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I've been getting severe headaches lately, and **insomnia** makes it hard for me to concentrate."
* **Role A:** "That sounds really tough. Could it be a **migraine** brought on by screen fatigue?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. My doctor suggested that I **rest in a dark, quiet room** during lunch breaks and reduce stress."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive health habits, clarify symptom details, or offer constructive advice politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly admitting health concerns, acknowledging insomnia, or sharing realistic medical habits. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion admissions of health anxiety, decline unverified medical advice, or report symptoms gently. |
| **It's a good idea to...** | A polite, non-intrusive formula for recommending health treatments or preventative care to others. |
| **You should try to...** | A supportive, professional way to suggest dietary or lifestyle adjustments, such as reducing salt intake. |
