# 36. Road and Street Moods

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parking space** | An individual designated area meant for parking a single vehicle | Паркувальне місце |
| **Parking lot / Car park** | A dedicated area or building used for parking multiple motor vehicles | Парковка, майданчик для паркування |
| **Pavement / Sidewalk** | A paved path for pedestrians at the side of a street or road | Тротуар |
| **Crossing / Crosswalk** | A marked area on a road where pedestrians have the right of way to cross | Пішохідний перехід |
| **Pothole** | A deep hole or pit in a road surface caused by traffic and weather wear | Яма / вибоїна (на дорозі) |
| **Pedestrian** | A person walking along a road or in a developed area rather than traveling in a vehicle | Пішохід |
| **Block the car** | To park a vehicle in a way that prevents another car from moving out | Заблокувати (запаркувати) авто |
| **Get stuck in traffic** | To be unable to move or travel smoothly due to heavy road congestion | Застрягнути в заторі |
| **Drive / Cross at a red light** | To pass through an intersection or street when the traffic signal indicates stop | Проїхати / перейти на червоне світло |
| **Take a wrong turn** | To make an incorrect directional turn while driving or walking | Повернути не туди / зробити помилковий поворот |
| **Hit the brakes suddenly** | To press down on a vehicle's braking pedal quickly and forcefully | Різко гальмувати / вдарити по гальмах |
| **Cut somebody off** | To drive unexpectedly in front of another vehicle without safe distance or warning | Підрізати когось (на дорозі) |
| **Push through a crowded crosswalk** | To force one's way quickly through a dense group of walking people | Проштовхуватися крізь натовп на переході |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "In American English, park your car in a **parking lot** and walk on the **sidewalk**; in British English, use a **car park** and stick to the **pavement**!"

> "When another driver **cuts you off**, stay calm and don't **hit the brakes suddenly** unless you have to!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Subject + Action Verb (cut off / get stuck in / hit the brakes) + Road Collocation`
Formula (US vs UK): `US: parking lot / sidewalk / crosswalk` vs `UK: car park / pavement / crossing`

* **Американські та британські варіанти дорожньої термінології (US vs UK Differences):**
  Важливо розрізняти лексику: в США вживають *parking lot*, *sidewalk*, *crosswalk*, тоді як у Великій Британії — *car park*, *pavement*, *crossing*.
* **Дієслівні сполучення (Road Action Collocations):**
  Усталені вирази описують поведінку водіїв та пішоходів (*get stuck in traffic, hit the brakes, cut someone off, drive/cross at a red light*).
* **Прийменники руху та місця (Prepositions of Place & Movement):**
  Вживаються конкретні прийменники з дорожньою лексикою: *in traffic* (у заторі), *on the road* (на дорозі), *at a red light* (на червоне світло), *through a crosswalk* (через перехід).

*Example: In London, people usually say car park, but in New York, they say parking lot.*
*Example: The driver hit the brakes suddenly when a cat ran into the road.*
*Example: We got stuck in traffic for two hours yesterday.*
*Example: Another driver cut me off on my way to work.*

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Choose the Correct Word (US vs UK & Road Terms)

1. In London, people usually say car park, but in New York, they say **parking lot**.
2. Many **pedestrians** don’t wait for the green light and cross the street anyway.
3. We got stuck in **traffic** for two hours yesterday.
4. There are so many **potholes** in the road that driving is dangerous.
5. The driver **hit the brakes suddenly** when a cat ran into the road.
6. He always **blocks my car** when I park in front of the office.

### Exercise 2: Contextual Choice Exercises

1. I was distracted and accidentally **drove through a red light**.
2. The pedestrian was in a hurry and decided to **cross at a red light** instead of waiting.
3. I couldn’t find a free **parking space** near the supermarket.
4. She took a **wrong turn** near the parking lot and got lost.
5. I **pushed through a crowded crosswalk** because I was in a rush.

### Exercise 3: True or False Concept Check

1. To take a wrong turn means to stop your car suddenly. — **FALSE**
2. If a driver cuts you off, it means they let you go first. — **FALSE**
3. Potholes are dangerous holes in the road. — **TRUE**
4. To cross at a red light is safe and correct. — **FALSE**
5. A parking space is the same as a parking lot. — **FALSE**
6. Pedestrians usually walk on sidewalks or pavements. — **TRUE**
7. Crossings are only for cars. — **FALSE**
8. If you get stuck in traffic, it means the cars are not moving. — **TRUE**

### Exercise 4: Complete the Dialogues

1. A: Why are you so late today?  
   B: I **got stuck in traffic**.
2. A: Oh no! You missed the turn.  
   B: Yes, I **took a wrong turn**.
3. A: What happened? Why did you stop so quickly?  
   B: A dog ran into the street, so I **hit the brakes suddenly**.

### Translation Block

* Ми застрягли у заторі на дві години вчора. — *We **got stuck in traffic** for two hours yesterday.*
* Водій різко натиснув на гальма, коли на дорогу вибіг кіт. — *The driver **hit the brakes suddenly** when a cat ran into the road.*
* На дорозі так багато вибоїн, що їздити небезпечно. — *There are so many **potholes** in the road that driving is dangerous.*
* Багато пішоходів не чекають на зелене світло і переходять вулицю на червоне. — *Many **pedestrians** don't wait for the green light and **cross at a red light**.*
* Інший водій підрізав мене біля пішохідного переходу. — *Another driver **cut me off** at the crossing.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: How do you usually react when you get stuck in traffic?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I take it as an opportunity to listen to an engaging podcast or practice my English listening skills.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my schedule; if I don't have pressing deadlines, I just stay patient and listen to music.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find getting stuck in traffic extremely frustrating, especially when I am rushing to an important meeting.

**Q: Have you ever taken a wrong turn while driving or walking in an unfamiliar city?**
* **Positive:** Actually, taking a wrong turn once led me to discover a wonderful hidden café in the city center.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally make a wrong turn, but GPS navigation apps make it easy to get back on track.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I get quite stressed when I take a wrong turn in heavy traffic because finding a place for a U-turn can be difficult.

**Q: What is the most annoying road behavior you observe in pedestrians or drivers?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I try to stay calm, but drivers who cut others off without signaling can create very dangerous situations.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, pedestrians crossing at a red light or blocking parking spaces bother me slightly, but I try to drive defensively.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I can't stand when people block my car in a parking lot, as it wastes a lot of time.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Explaining a Delay for an Office Meeting**
* **Context:** A project manager explaining their late arrival at an executive meeting due to road conditions.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! We were getting slightly worried. Did you experience any issues on your way to the office?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. I **got stuck in traffic** for over forty minutes because of roadwork near the central bridge."
* **Role A:** "Ah, that area is notorious for congestion. Did you manage to find a **parking space** easily?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, someone **blocked my car** near the entrance, so I had to spend an extra ten minutes finding a spot in the secondary **car park**."

**Scenario 2: Commuting and Road Safety Discussion**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing daily commuting hazards and city road infrastructure.
* **Role A:** "How was your drive this morning? The weather conditions seemed quite challenging."
* **Role B:** "Actually, a driver **cut me off** near the main **crosswalk**, so I had to **hit the brakes suddenly**."
* **Role A:** "That sounds scary! Are the city roads in good condition along your route?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, no. There are numerous **potholes** on the main street, which makes driving quite dangerous for both drivers and **pedestrians**."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive explanations, share unexpected road experiences, or clarify delays politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly admitting driving mistakes, acknowledging road frustration, or sharing neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion complaints about heavy traffic, bad road infrastructure, or late arrivals gracefully. |
| **I got stuck in traffic** | A standard, courteous formula for explaining delays to colleagues or clients without sounding unorganized. |
| **I apologize for the delay** | Used in professional environments when road issues cause late attendance at meetings. |
