# 03. Your Life Checklist

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bucket list** | A list of things a person wants to do, achieve, or experience during their lifetime | Список бажань / цілей на життя |
| **Must-see** | A place, landmark, or phenomenon that is so remarkable that everyone should visit it | Те, що обов'язково треба побачити |
| **Must-read** | A book, article, or document that is highly recommended as essential reading | Те, що обов'язково треба прочитати |
| **Must-try** | A special food, activity, or experience that is strongly recommended to try | Те, що обов'язково варто спробувати |
| **Must-have** | An item or tool that is essential, highly popular, or necessary to own | Незамінна річ, обов'язкова для володіння |
| **See the Northern Lights** | To observe the colorful aurora borealis in northern sky regions like Iceland or Norway | Побачити Північне сяйво |
| **Hike a famous trail** | To go on a long outdoor walk along a renowned natural path (e.g., Machu Picchu trail) | Здійснити похід відомою стежкою |
| **Experience a hot air balloon ride** | To travel through the sky in a basket suspended beneath a heated balloon | Політати на гарячій повітряній кулі |
| **Go bungee jumping** | To jump from a high structure while attached to a large elastic cord | Стрибнути з банджі (з мосту/вишки) |
| **Attend a major sports event** | To be present as a spectator at a significant athletic competition | Відвідати видатну спортивну подію |
| **Stay in an overwater bungalow** | To lodge in a thatched-roof bungalow built directly over ocean or lagoon waters | Проживати у бунгало над водою |
| **Go on a wildlife safari** | To take an expedition to observe wild animals in their natural habitat | Вирушити на дике сафарі |
| **Visit all the wonders of the world** | To travel to renowned historical landmarks (e.g., Colosseum, Great Wall, Pyramids) | Відвідати всі дива світу |
| **Take a solo travel trip** | To journey alone to explore new places and develop personal independence | Вирушити у соло-подорож |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Create a **bucket list** to chase your dreams; mark every **must-see** place and **must-try** dish along the way!"

> "A reliable backpack is a **must-have** when you **take a solo travel trip** to **visit all the wonders of the world**."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Compound Adjective = Must + Verb (e.g., must-see, must-read, must-try, must-have)`
Formula: `Subject + have / has + always wanted to + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula: `Subject + is + at the top of my bucket list / one of my dreams`

* **Складені прикметники (Compound Adjectives):** Утворюються за моделлю *must + дієслово* (через дефіс). Вони використовуються для опису об'єктів, визначних місць, книжок або страв, які є настільки якісними або вражаючими, що їх рекомендується відвідати, прочитати чи спробувати кожному.
* **Конструкції для вираження мрій та цілей:**
  * *I've always wanted to...* (Я завжди хотів/хотіла...)
  * *One of my dreams is to...* (Одна з моїх мрій — це...)
  * *It would be amazing to...* (Було б дивовижно...)
  * *It’s at the top of my bucket list because...* (Це на самому верху мого списку бажань, тому що...)

*Example: The Grand Canyon is a must-see destination in the USA.*
*Example: Pizza and pasta are a must-try if you travel to Italy.*
*Example: A good dictionary is a must-have for every English student.*
*Example: I've always wanted to see the Northern Lights in Iceland.*
*Example: Staying in an overwater bungalow in Bora Bora is at the top of my bucket list.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What is at the top of your bucket list?**
* **Positive:** Actually, seeing the Northern Lights in Iceland is at the very top of my bucket list because I've always wanted to experience that magical natural phenomenon.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I haven't written down a formal bucket list yet, but hiking a famous trail in the mountains sounds like a great activity for next summer.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really keep a bucket list, as I prefer focusing on immediate goals rather than long-term travel plans.

**Q: What is a must-have item when you travel?**
* **Positive:** Actually, a high-quality set of noise-canceling headphones is a complete must-have for me, especially during long international flights.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, a compact power bank and a durable backpack are usually all the must-have items I need for a weekend trip.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't carry any special must-have travel gadgets, as I prefer packing as lightly as possible.

**Q: What is a must-see destination or landmark in your country or abroad?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'd say visiting the ancient city of Petra in Jordan is an absolute must-see for anyone interested in history and architecture.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, the historical city center in Kyiv is quite an impressive must-see if you happen to be visiting Ukraine.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I couldn't single out just one must-see landmark, as different travelers enjoy very different types of sights.

**Q: What is a dish or local food that you consider a must-try?**
* **Positive:** Actually, trying authentic traditional Ukrainian borscht with garlic pampushky is a total must-try for every international visitor.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, fresh seafood is a reasonable must-try whenever you travel to coastal Mediterranean regions.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I'm not much of a food enthusiast, so I don't have a specific must-try dish to recommend right now.

**Q: Have you ever considered taking a solo travel trip?**
* **Positive:** Actually, taking a solo travel trip around Southern Europe was one of the most empowering and memorable experiences of my life.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I have thought about taking a brief solo trip, though I generally prefer traveling with close friends or family.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I wouldn't enjoy a solo travel trip, as I find exploring new cities much more fun when sharing the moment with others.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Planning an Executive Team Retreat**
* **Context:** Two HR managers discussing potential locations and activities for an annual executive retreat.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss the destination for our upcoming corporate team-building retreat? Do you have any special recommendations?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest booking an eco-resort where team members can experience a **hot air balloon ride** or **hike a famous trail** together."
* **Role A:** "That sounds wonderful! Would a trip like that fit our budget, or is it too expensive?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, staying in luxury accommodation might be pricey, but visiting key natural sights would certainly be a **must-see** experience for everyone."

**Scenario 2: Client Hospitality & Travel Recommendations**
* **Context:** A travel consultant advising a business client on their upcoming trip and leisure options.
* **Role A:** "Could you tell me if there are any special recommendations for my stay in Cappadocia after the conference?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, booking a boutique cave hotel is a complete **must-have** experience, and watching the sunrise from a hot air balloon is a total **must-see**."
* **Role A:** "Thank you! What about local cuisine? Is there anything specific I should order?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, the local pottery kebab is an absolute **must-try** dish that every visitor highly recommends."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce exciting plans, share personal bucket list goals, or add positive details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when sharing modest travel preferences, expressing neutral opinions, or discussing budget constraints gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to express polite regret when declining extreme activities (like bungee jumping) or admitting lack of travel time. |
| **I've always wanted to...** | A courteous, natural formula for expressing long-held personal dreams or bucket list ambitions. |
| **It would be amazing to...** | Used to describe hypothetical dream vacations or bucket list goals in a soft, non-demanding way. |
| **I'd recommend that you...** | A respectful way to give travel suggestions, recommending must-see places or must-try foods to colleagues. |
