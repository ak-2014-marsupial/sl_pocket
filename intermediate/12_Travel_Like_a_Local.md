# 12. Travel Like a Local

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stay in a guesthouse** | To lodge or reside in a small, privately owned house for guests rather than a large hotel | Зупинитися в гостьовому будинку |
| **Be flexible with your plans** | To be adaptable and open to changing one's schedule or arrangements | Бути гнучким щодо своїх планів |
| **Know alternative routes** | To be familiar with secondary or different paths to avoid traffic or obstacles | Знати альтернативні маршрути |
| **Visit landmarks** | To go see famous, recognizable, or historical sights in a city | Відвідати визначні памʼятки |
| **Have a memorable experience** | To have an event or activity that leaves a strong, lasting, and pleasant impression | Мати незабутній досвід |
| **Quiet district** | A peaceful area or neighborhood located away from noisy city centers | Тихий район |
| **Accommodation** | A place where travelers stay or live temporarily (such as hotels, hostels, or apartments) | Житло, проживання |
| **Real neighborhoods** | Authentic residential areas where local residents live, away from tourist crowds | Справжні (нетуристичні) райони |
| **Hidden gems** | Special, beautiful, or interesting places that are not widely known to tourists | Приховані «перлини» |
| **Tourist attractions** | Popular sights or places that draw a large number of visitors and tourists | Туристичні памʼятки |
| **Off-the-beaten spots** | Unconventional, non-touristy places located away from popular tourist routes | Нетуристичні місця (поза протоптаними доріжками) |
| **Authentic** | Genuine, original, and traditional to local culture rather than made for tourists | Справжній, автентичний |
| **Affordable** | Reasonably priced, inexpensive, and providing good value for money | Доступний за ціною |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Travel to **off-the-beaten spots** to discover **hidden gems** and taste **authentic** local food!"

> "Stay in an **affordable guesthouse**, **be flexible with your plans**, and explore **real neighborhoods** like a local."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Giving Travel Advice): `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + can / should + Verb (Infinitive)`  
Formula (Prepositional Travel Phrases): `stay in + Accommodation` / `be flexible with + Plans` / `in case of + Noun`  

* **Конструкція реальної умови та поради (If + Present Simple, Subject + can/should + V):**  
  Використовується для надання практичних порад мандрівникам. Головна частина містить модальне дієслово *can* або *should* для вираження можливості чи рекомендації (*If you are flexible with your plans, you can enjoy your trip more*).
* **Вживання прийменників та фразових сполучень у подорожах:**  
  * **Stay in** a guesthouse / hotel / hostel (*зупинятися в...*).  
  * **Be flexible with** your plans (*бути гнучким щодо...*).  
  * **In case of** delays / traffic jams (*у випадку...*).  

*Example: If you don't want to spend too much money, try to find something affordable.*  
*Example: It's always good to know alternative routes in case of traffic.*  
*Example: Staying in a guesthouse can be a memorable experience.*

* Якщо ви не хочете витрачати занадто багато грошей, спробуйте знайти щось доступне за ціною. — *If you don't want to spend too much money, try to find something **affordable**.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you prefer visiting popular tourist attractions or off-the-beaten spots? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'd say I much prefer off-the-beaten spots because you can avoid crowds and discover genuine hidden gems.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the destination; I visit major landmarks on my first visit, but then I look for quieter areas.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really enjoy off-the-beaten spots, as I feel safer and more comfortable visiting famous tourist attractions.

**Q: What kind of accommodation do you usually choose when you travel? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always choose an affordable guesthouse in a quiet district so I can experience how locals live.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my budget; I stay in guesthouses for casual trips, but book hotel accommodation for business travel.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't like staying in guesthouses, as I prefer standard hotel amenities and predictable services.

**Q: What was the most memorable experience you've had while traveling?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my most memorable experience was discovering an authentic local restaurant in a real neighborhood where the chef cooked traditional meals.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, every trip brings a memorable experience, whether it's visiting famous landmarks or trying new street food.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't had many memorable travel experiences recently because my schedule hasn't allowed for flexible planning.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discussing Travel Plans & Accommodation Options**
* **Context:** Two colleagues planning an upcoming trip with an optional weekend stay.
* **Role A:** "Are you planning to stay in the city center during our conference next week?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I decided to book an **affordable guesthouse** in a **quiet district** rather than a busy hotel."
* **Role A:** "That sounds nice! Will it be easy to reach the conference venue from there?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. My host showed me **alternative routes** so I can avoid morning traffic jams."

**Scenario 2: Sharing Cultural Travel Recommendations**
* **Context:** A colleague asking for travel advice before visiting an overseas city.
* **Role A:** "Could you recommend some places to visit in Rome? I'm hoping to avoid overcrowded locations."
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest exploring **real neighborhoods** and looking for **hidden gems** instead of spending all your time at **tourist attractions**."
* **Role A:** "That's very helpful advice! What about dining options?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, if you want **authentic** local food, avoid restaurants right next to major **landmarks** and ask locals for **off-the-beaten spots**."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive travel recommendations, clarify plans, or share personal experiences politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing candid preferences about accommodation, budget choices, or travel habits gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion negative answers, express hesitation about crowded places, or explain travel limitations gently. |
| **I'd suggest that you...** | A polite, helpful formula for giving travel recommendations to colleagues or friends. |
| **It allows you to...** | Used to explain the practical benefits of staying in guesthouses or exploring off-the-beaten spots. |
