# 06. How to Optimize Your Work

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plan ahead** | To arrange or organize activities well before they happen | Планувати заздалегідь |
| **Set priorities** | To decide which tasks are most important and should be done first | Встановлювати пріоритети |
| **Use AI tools** | To utilize artificial intelligence applications to assist with daily tasks | Використовувати ШІ-інструменти |
| **Take breaks** | To stop working for a short period to rest and restore energy | Робити перерви |
| **Filter information** | To select useful details and ignore irrelevant or unnecessary data | Фільтрувати інформацію |
| **Convert text to speech** | To transform written words into spoken audio using software | Перетворювати текст на мовлення |
| **Sync across devices** | To make files and data automatically identical across all computers and phones | Синхронізувати між пристроями |
| **Automate tasks** | To let a computer program or machine perform routine work instead of doing it manually | Автоматизувати завдання |
| **Set goals** | To define specific targets or achievements for the future | Ставити цілі |
| **Use reminders** | To set up digital alerts so you do not forget important events or meetings | Використовувати нагадування |
| **Generate ideas** | To brainstorm or produce new creative thoughts, solutions, and plans | Генерувати ідеї |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "To avoid overload and keep your mind clear, **set priorities** first and **filter information** second!"

> "Good leaders **plan ahead** and **automate tasks**, while smart workers remember to **take breaks** and **sync across devices**."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Imperative: Verb + Object`
Formula (Advice): `It is + Adjective + to + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula (Modal Advice): `Subject + should + Verb (Infinitive)`

* **Наказовий спосіб (Imperatives):** Використовується для надання прямих порад, інструкцій та алгоритмів оптимізації роботи (*Plan ahead, Set priorities, Take breaks*).
* **Конструкції з інфінітивом (Infinitives of Purpose & Evaluation):** Вживаються для пояснення доцільності дій або мети їх виконання (*It's smart to plan ahead..., To finish projects on time, it's important to...*).
* **Модальні дієслова для порад (Modal Verbs for Advice):** Використання дієслова *should* дозволяє висловити м'яку рекомендацію щодо покращення продуктивності (*You should set goals for the future*).

*Example: Plan ahead before starting a complex project to avoid unnecessary stress.*
*Example: It is important to set priorities when managing tight deadlines.*
*Example: You should automate routine tasks like sending weekly status updates.*
*Example: Remember to take breaks during the day to stay focused and productive.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you usually plan ahead before starting a major project?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say I always plan ahead because setting a structured timeline helps me finish projects on time without last-minute stress.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the project size; I plan ahead for large initiatives, but I handle small daily tasks as they come.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely get the chance to plan ahead, to be honest, as unexpected client requests usually disrupt my schedule.

**Q: How do you set priorities when you have too many urgent tasks at once?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I use a digital planner to evaluate deadlines and business impact, which makes it easy to set priorities clearly.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I simply address tasks with the nearest deadline first and push less critical discussions to another day.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find it quite challenging to set priorities under pressure, so I often end up feeling slightly overwhelmed.

**Q: Do you use AI tools or reminders to automate tasks in your daily routine?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I rely on AI tools daily to generate ideas and automate routine emails, which saves a lot of valuable working hours.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I regularly use digital calendar reminders, but I haven't fully explored advanced AI tools for my work yet.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't use AI tools or automated software very often because I prefer managing my tasks manually.

**Q: Do you manage to take regular breaks during a demanding workday?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I make it a habit to take short five-minute breaks every hour, as it keeps my mind fresh and focused.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I try to step away from my desk for lunch, though during busy sprints, I sometimes skip short breaks.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely take breaks when deadlines are tight, which often leaves me feeling completely drained by the end of the day.

**Q: Is it easy for you to filter information when receiving a high volume of emails?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I filter information effectively by skimming key bullet points and setting automated email rules for primary topics.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I manage to process critical messages, though reading long reports can still take quite a bit of time.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I struggle to filter information quickly, to be honest, because every incoming notification seems urgent.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Optimizing Team Workflow & Setting Priorities**
* **Context:** A team lead and a project manager discussing ways to improve efficiency during a busy project phase.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss our team's current productivity? We seem to be struggling with tight deadlines this month."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, the team is getting overwhelmed by routine emails. I'd suggest we **set priorities** for critical deliverables first."
* **Role A:** "That makes sense. Would it be helpful if we began to **automate tasks** like status reporting and **use AI tools** for research?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, that would be fantastic. It would allow everyone to focus on strategic work and remember to **take breaks** regularly."

**Scenario 2: Data Management & Syncing Across Devices**
* **Context:** A senior developer assisting a new colleague with software configuration and remote file access.
* **Role A:** "Would you mind helping me with my workspace setup? I can't access my presentation files on my tablet."
* **Role B:** "Actually, you just need to **sync across devices** through our secure cloud portal so your data updates automatically."
* **Role A:** "Thank you! Also, is there a quick way to review these lengthy technical manuals?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I usually **convert text to speech** and listen to them while reviewing code, which saves a lot of time."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share helpful software tips, suggest positive workflow solutions, or add details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when acknowledging work pressure, sharing candid personal habits, or admitting workload challenges. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to express polite regret when struggling with deadlines, interruptions, or information overload. |
| **I'd suggest that we...** | A professional, courteous way to offer recommendations for optimizing team tasks or workflows. |
| **Would it be helpful if...** | An indirect, respectful formula for proposing new tools, automation options, or process adjustments. |
| **It is smart / important to...** | Used to give polite, professional advice regarding productivity, planning, and task management. |
