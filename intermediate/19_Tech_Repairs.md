# 19. Tech Repairs

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fix a cracked screen** | To repair or replace a damaged glass display on a mobile device | Полагодити тріснутий екран |
| **Replace a phone screen** | To install a new display assembly on a smartphone | Замінити екран телефону |
| **Reset the phone to factory settings** | To restore a device to its original system state, erasing all user data | Скинути телефон до заводських налаштувань |
| **Replace a phone case** | To switch an old protective cover for a new one | Замінити чохол для телефону |
| **Apply a screen protector** | To attach a thin protective film or tempered glass onto a display | Наклеїти захисну плівку / скло |
| **Shockproof phone case** | A heavy-duty cover designed to protect a phone from drops and impacts | Ударостійкий чохол |
| **Waterproof phone case** | A sealed enclosure that prevents water from entering and damaging a phone | Водонепроникний чохол |
| **Unlock the phone** | To remove the password lock or carrier restriction on a device | Розблокувати телефон |
| **Service the car** | To perform routine maintenance and inspection on a vehicle | Провести техогляд / обслуговувати авто |
| **Change tires** | To replace worn or seasonal tires on a vehicle | Змінити шини |
| **Fix brakes** | To repair or replace damaged brake pads and rotors for safe stopping | Відремонтувати гальма |
| **Remove a dent** | To straighten out a sunken area on a car's bodywork | Видалити вм'ятину |
| **Check the engine** | To inspect the motor for mechanical issues or warning indicators | Перевірити двигун |
| **Remove a scratch** | To buff out or paint over surface marks on a vehicle or device | Видалити подряпину |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Always **apply a screen protector** and use a **shockproof phone case** before you drop your brand new phone!"

> "Before selling your old device, make sure to **reset the phone to factory settings** so none of your personal data remains!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Verbs of Repair & Maintenance): `Subject + need to / have to / should + Verb (Infinitive) + Object`  
Formula (Purpose & Prevention): `Subject + Verb + Object + to avoid / in order to + Verb (Infinitive)`  
Formula (Condition & Maintenance): `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + must / should + Verb (Infinitive)`  

* **Дієслова відновлення та обслуговування (Verbs of Repair & Maintenance):**  
  Дієслова *fix, replace, reset, service, change, remove, apply* описують конкретні дії з технічного обслуговування гаджетів чи автомобілів.
* **Вираження потреби та необхідності (Expressing Necessity):**  
  Конструкції *need to*, *have to*, та *should* вживаються для вказування на необхідність проведення ремонту (*I need to fix a cracked screen*).
* **Умовні застереження для збереження техніки (Preventative Action Rules):**  
  Підрядні речення умови (*If-clauses*) описують рекомендації для запобігання пошкодженням (*If you drop your phone often, buy a shockproof phone case*).

*Example: I dropped my phone on the pavement and now I need to fix a cracked screen.*  
*Example: The technician recommended that we reset the phone to factory settings.*  
*Example: We always service the car before going on long road trips.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What do you usually do first when you drop your phone and crack the screen?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if I crack the screen, I immediately take it to an authorized service center to replace the phone screen with an original part.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on how severe the damage is; if it's just a minor scratch, I apply a screen protector over it and keep using it.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I'm quite clumsy with gadgets, to be honest, which is why I always buy a shockproof phone case in advance.

**Q: Have you ever had to reset your phone to factory settings because of a software glitch?**
* **Positive:** Actually, resetting the phone to factory settings completely resolved my system lag, though I made sure to back up my photos first.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I try to avoid factory resets because restoring all apps takes time, but occasionally it's necessary.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I felt quite stressed when I had to reset my phone, as I accidentally lost some unbacked-up contacts.

**Q: How often do you service your car or change tires for different seasons?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I schedule routine maintenance to service the car twice a year and always change tires before the winter season begins.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I rely on my garage mechanic to remind me when it's time to check the engine or fix brakes.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid maintaining a vehicle can be quite costly, especially when you have to remove a dent after a minor parking mistake.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Visiting a Tech Repair Shop**
* **Context:** A customer bringing a damaged smartphone to a repair technician.
* **Role A:** "Good afternoon! I dropped my smartphone on the asphalt this morning, and the display isn't responding."
* **Role B:** "Actually, looking at the damage, we'll need to **replace the phone screen** completely."
* **Role A:** "I see. Will I need to **reset the phone to factory settings**, or will my personal data remain safe?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, your data will stay intact. Once we finish the repair, I'd also recommend that you **apply a screen protector** and use a **shockproof phone case**."

**Scenario 2: Discussing Vehicle Maintenance at a Service Center**
* **Context:** A car owner consulting a mechanic before a long business trip.
* **Role A:** "Hello! I have a long cross-country drive next week, so I'd like to **service the car** before leaving."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, that's very wise. We'll **check the engine**, inspect the fluid levels, and **fix brakes** if necessary."
* **Role A:** "Great. Could you also check if it's time to **change tires** and see if you can **remove a scratch** on the passenger door?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! We can polish out the scratch and ensure your tires are in optimal condition for the journey."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce technical solutions, confirm repair options, or clarify device issues politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly explaining repair costs, admitting minor driver mistakes, or giving neutral technical advice. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion news about severe hardware damage, delayed parts, or costly automotive repairs gracefully. |
| **I'd recommend that you...** | A polite, professional formula for suggesting protective measures like shockproof cases or regular car servicing. |
| **It is important to...** | Used in professional technical advice to emphasize safety checks like fixing brakes or backing up data. |
