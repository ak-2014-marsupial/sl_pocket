# 17. Indirect Questions

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct question** | A straightforward question asking for information directly (e.g., "Where is the station?") | Пряме запитання |
| **Indirect question** | A polite, cushioned way of asking for information using introductory phrases | Непряме (ввічливе) запитання |
| **Sound rude** | To appear impolite or abrupt in conversation without intending to be | Звучати грубо / неввічливо |
| **Introductory phrase** | A polite opening expression placed at the beginning of an indirect question | Вступна ввічлива фраза |
| **Direct word order** | Standard affirmative sentence order (Subject + Verb) used in indirect questions | Прямий порядок слів |
| **Do you happen to know...?** | A polite expression used when you aren't sure if the person has the information | Чи бува ви не знаєте...? |
| **Would you mind telling me...?** | A highly polite formula used to request information without being intrusive | Чи не могли б ви сказати мені...? |
| **I was wondering if...** | A soft, indirect expression used to ask for assistance or information gracefully | Мені цікаво / Я хотів дізнатися, чи... |
| **Do you mind me asking...?** | A cautious phrase used before asking personal or sensitive questions | Ви не проти, якщо я запитаю...? |
| **Whether / If** | Conjunctions used to introduce indirect Yes/No questions | Чи (сполучник умови/вибору) |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "To avoid **sounding rude** in English, transform direct questions into polite **indirect questions** using soft introductory phrases!"

> "When using **indirect questions**, remember to switch to **direct word order**—put the subject before the verb and drop *do/does/did*!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Wh-Questions): `Polite Phrase + Wh-word (where/when/why/how) + Subject + Verb`  
Formula (Yes/No Questions): `Polite Phrase + if / whether + Subject + Verb`  

* **Призначення та концепція (Usage & Tone):**  
  Прямі запитання (*Where is the bank? What do you want?*) можуть здаватися носіям мови надто різкими або грубими. Щоб звучати ввічливо та професійно, вживаються **непрямі запитання (Indirect Questions)**.

* **Головне правило порядку слів (Direct Word Order Rule):**  
  Після вступної ввічливої фрази порядок слів стає **прямим (підмет + присудок)**, як у звичайному стверджувальному реченні!  
  * Допоміжні дієслова *do*, *does*, *did* **вилучаються** (*Where does the train arrive?* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Could you tell me where the train arrives?*).  
  * Дієслово *to be* та модальні дієслова стають **після підмета** (*Where is the bank?* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Do you know where the bank is?*).

* **Питання типу Yes/No (If / Whether):**  
  Якщо у прямому питанні немає питального слова (*Wh-word*), у непрямому питанні додається сполучник **if** або **whether** (*Is the shop open?* \\(\rightarrow\\) *Do you happen to know if the shop is open?*).

* **Популярні вступні фрази (Useful Introductory Phrases):**  
  * *Can / Could you tell me...*  
  * *Do you know... / Do you happen to know...*  
  * *Can I ask you... / Do you mind me asking...*  
  * *I wonder... / I was wondering if...*  
  * *Would you mind telling me... / I'd like to know...*

*Example: Direct: Where is the nearest bank? \\(\rightarrow\\) Indirect: Could you tell me where the nearest bank is?*  
*Example: Direct: What time does the film start? \\(\rightarrow\\) Indirect: Do you know what time the film starts?*  
*Example: Direct: Can you help me with this task? \\(\rightarrow\\) Indirect: I was wondering if you could help me with this task.*  
*Example: Direct: Is the shop open on Sundays? \\(\rightarrow\\) Indirect: Do you happen to know whether the shop is open on Sundays?*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Transform Direct Questions into Indirect Questions

1. Where is the nearest supermarket?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) Could you tell me **where the nearest supermarket is**?
2. What time does the meeting start?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) Do you know **what time the meeting starts**?
3. Where did you buy that laptop?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) I was wondering **where you bought that laptop**.
4. How much does this ticket cost?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) Would you mind telling me **how much this ticket costs**?
5. Why are you learning English?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) Can I ask you **why you are learning English**?
6. Is the museum open today?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) Do you happen to know **if the museum is open today**?
7. When will the results be announced?  
   \\(\rightarrow\\) I'd like to know **when the results will be announced**.

### Translation Block

* Чи не могли б ви сказати мені, де знаходиться найближчий банк? — *Could you tell me **where the nearest bank is**?*
* Ви випадково не знаєте, чи аптека відкрита сьогодні? — *Do you happen to know **if the pharmacy is open today**?*
* Я б хотів дізнатися, о котрій годині розпочинається зустріч. — *I'd like to know **what time the meeting starts**.*
* Чи ви не проти, якщо я запитаю, де ви навчилися так добре розмовляти англійською? — *Do you mind me asking **where you learned to speak English so well**?*
* Мені цікаво, чи ви могли б допомогти мені з цим завданням. — *I was wondering **if you could help me with this task**.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Have you ever sounded rude in English without meaning to because you used a direct question?**
* **Positive:** Actually, yes; when I first started communicating with foreign clients, I asked "What do you want?" instead of "Could you tell me what you need?", which sounded a bit abrupt.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually try to add "please" to direct questions, but learning indirect questions has made my speech much smoother.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I've always been quite cautious with politeness, so I regularly use phrases like "I was wondering if..." to keep conversation polite.

**Q: Which introductory phrase do you use most often when asking strangers for directions in a city?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always use "Could you tell me where... is?" because it is polite, natural, and easy for locals to understand.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the situation; I use "Excuse me, do you know where..." for quick questions on the street.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I used to get nervous asking strangers, but using "Do you happen to know if..." gives a very soft entry into conversation.

**Q: Is it common in your company culture to use indirect questions during formal meetings?**
* **Positive:** Actually, yes; in our international team meetings, everyone uses "Would you mind telling me..." when asking for project updates.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, internal team chats are fairly casual, but we switch to polite indirect questions when speaking with executive clients.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid some team members still rely on direct translation from their native language, which can occasionally sound a bit direct.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Asking for Assistance in a New Workplace**
* **Context:** A newly hired specialist asking a colleague for administrative and technical guidance politely.
* **Role A:** "Excuse me, Alex! Do you have a moment? I'm trying to locate the project documentation."
* **Role B:** "Sure! How can I help you out?"
* **Role A:** "Actually, **I was wondering if you could tell me where** the shared drive files are stored."
* **Role B:** "Of course! I'll send you the direct link. **Do you happen to know whether** you have access permissions yet?"
* **Role A:** "To be honest, I'm not sure. **Could you tell me who** I should contact if I need access?"
* **Role B:** "You can contact IT support directly. They'll set it up in five minutes."

**Scenario 2: Clarifying Client Project Requirements**
* **Context:** A account manager confirming project timelines and budgets with a client during a video call.
* **Role A:** "Thank you for joining today's project review call. We'd like to confirm a few details before kickoff."
* **Role B:** "Sounds good! What information do you need from our end?"
* **Role A:** "Actually, **would you mind telling me when** your team expects the initial prototype deliverable?"
* **Role B:** "We are aiming for the end of Q3. Is that timeline feasible for your engineering team?"
* **Role A:** "Yes, absolutely! Also, **can I ask you whether** there are any specific brand guidelines we should follow?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes; our brand manager will email you the design system guide today."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Could you tell me...?** | Standard, highly versatile phrase for asking for directions, times, or general information politely. |
| **I was wondering if...** | Extremely polite, soft formula used to ask for favors, assistance, or sensitive checks. |
| **Do you happen to know...?** | Ideal when asking strangers or colleagues for information they might not necessarily possess. |
| **Would you mind telling me...?** | Professional and formal softener used in business meetings, client calls, or official emails. |
| **Do you mind me asking...?** | Used before asking slightly personal, background, or salary-related questions gently. |
| **Can I ask you...?** | A polite, friendly way to introduce a question during casual or professional discussions. |
