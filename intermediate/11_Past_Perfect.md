# 11. Past Perfect

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Perfect** | A verb tense used to express an action completed before another past action or time | Минулий доконаний час |
| **Before** | At an earlier time; preceding an action or moment in the past | Перед тим як / раніше |
| **After** | In the time following an event or completed action | Після того як |
| **By the time** | At or before the moment that another past event occurred | До того часу як |
| **As soon as** | Immediately at or after the moment that something occurred | Як тільки |
| **Because (showing cause)** | For the reason that; explaining the earlier cause of a past state | Оскільки / тому що |
| **Break up with someone** | To end a romantic relationship with someone | Розійтися / розірвати стосунки |
| **Skip breakfast / meals** | To intentionally or accidentally miss eating a meal | Пропустити сніданок / прийом їжі |
| **Arrive at the station / party** | To reach a destination or venue in the past | Прибути на станцію / вечірку |
| **Lose one's phone / wallet** | To misplace or be unable to find an essential personal item | Загубити телефон / гаманець |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Past Perfect is the **earlier past**: when two actions happened in the past, use **had + V3** for the one that happened first!"

> "Connect past events clearly with signal words: **before**, **after**, **by the time**, **as soon as**, and **because**!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Affirmative): `Subject + had + Verb+ed / Verb3`  
Formula (Negative): `Subject + had not (hadn't) + Verb+ed / Verb3`  
Formula (Question): `Had + Subject + Verb+ed / Verb3 ?`  
Formula (Time Sequence): `Action 1 (Earlier) = Past Perfect (had + V3) | Action 2 (Later) = Past Simple (V2/ed)`  

* **Визначення та призначення (Usage):**  
  Час **Past Perfect** (минулий доконаний) використовується для опису дії, яка відбулася **раніше за іншу дію або певний момент у минулому** («перед-минуле»). Якщо у минулому відбулися дві події, Past Perfect завжди позначає першу (давнішу) дію.
* **Маркери часу (Signal Words):**  
  * **before** — *She had studied English before she moved to London.*  
  * **after** — *After he had eaten, he went for a walk.*  
  * **by the time** — *By the time we got to the cinema, the film had started.*  
  * **as soon as** — *As soon as he had broken up with her, she left the house.*  
  * **when** — *When I arrived, she had already gone home.*  
  * **because (причина)** — *He was tired because he had worked all day.*  

*Example: When I arrived at the party, Tom wasn't there. He had gone home.*  
*Example: She was tired because she hadn't slept well the night before.*  
*Example: By the time the train arrived, we had already left.*  
*Example: He couldn't pay because he had forgotten his wallet at home.*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Open the Brackets in Past Perfect

1. By the time the train arrived, we **had left**.
2. She was tired because she **had worked** a lot.
3. He couldn’t pay because he **had forgotten** his wallet at home.
4. I was hungry because I **had skipped** breakfast.
5. I couldn’t call you because I **had lost** my phone.
6. After she **had finished** her homework, she went to bed.

### Exercise 2: Build Sentences in Past Perfect

1. We were late. (the film / already / start) \\(\rightarrow\\) **The film had already started.**
2. He was nervous. (he / not / study / for the test) \\(\rightarrow\\) **He hadn’t studied for the test.**
3. They were very tired. (they / walk /) \\(\rightarrow\\) **They had walked.**
4. We were excited. (we / never / be / to London before) \\(\rightarrow\\) **We had never been to London before.**

### Exercise 3: Past Simple or Past Perfect

1. When we **arrived** at the station, the train **had already left**.
2. She **was** very tired because she **had not slept** well the night before.
3. After he **had finished** his work, he **went** out with friends.
4. They **did not know** that we **had already bought** the tickets.
5. I **forgot** that I **had met** her before.
6. The teacher **checked** the homework that the students **had done**.
7. When I **saw** him, I knew I **had never met** him before.
8. She **could not enter** the house because she **had lost** the key.
9. By the time we **got** to the cinema, the film **had started**.
10. He **told** me that he **had visited** London twice.
11. The storm **had already stopped** when we **went** outside.
12. They **had not eaten** anything all day, so they **were** very hungry.

### Exercise 4: Combine 2 Sentences into 1

1. He ate dinner. / He washed his hands. \\(\rightarrow\\) **By the time he ate dinner, he had washed his hands.**
2. She drank coffee. / She went to work. \\(\rightarrow\\) **Before she went to work, she had drunk coffee.**
3. He bought a new phone. / He lost his old one. \\(\rightarrow\\) **After he had lost his phone, he bought a new one.**
4. I put on my shoes. / I went outside. \\(\rightarrow\\) **Before I went outside, I had put on my shoes.**
5. I read the book. / I told my friend about it. \\(\rightarrow\\) **After I had read the book, I told my friend about it.**

### Translation Block

* Коли я прийшов на вечірку, Том уже пішов додому. — *When I arrived at the party, Tom **had already gone** home.*
* Вона була втомлена, тому що працювала весь день. — *She was tired because she **had worked** all day.*
* До того часу як ми дісталися кінотеатру, фільм уже розпочався. — *By the time we got to the cinema, the film **had started**.*
* Він не міг розплатитися, тому що забув гаманець удома. — *He couldn't pay because he **had forgotten** his wallet at home.*
* Після того як вона закінчила домашнє завдання, вона лягла спати. — *After she **had finished** her homework, she went to bed.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Have you ever arrived at an event or airport and realized you had forgotten something crucial?**
* **Positive:** Actually, when I arrived at the airport last year, I realized I had left my boarding pass at home, but fortunately I managed to retrieve an electronic copy.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I arrived at a client presentation once and realized I had forgotten my charger, though a colleague kindly lent me theirs.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I had never experienced such an oversight until last month, when I arrived at a meeting and realized I had lost my presentation drive.

**Q: Tell me about a time when you were extremely tired because you had worked too hard.**
* **Positive:** Actually, after our team had completed a major quarterly deployment, I felt exhausted because we had worked non-stop for three days.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I was quite tired yesterday because I had spent the entire morning reviewing financial documents.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I try to avoid overworking, though I felt drained last week because I hadn't slept well before a project launch.

**Q: What was a place you visited recently that you had never seen before?**
* **Positive:** Actually, when I traveled to London last summer, I visited historical sites that I had only ever seen in books before.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I visited a new exhibition center downtown; I hadn't heard much about it before, but it turned out to be very engaging.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't traveled much recently, so I haven't visited any places I hadn't seen before.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Explaining a Project Delay & System Outage**
* **Context:** A project lead explaining an unexpected project bottleneck to a client.
* **Role A:** "Could you clarify why yesterday's software update was delayed by two hours?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, by the time our engineers initiated the deployment, our server **had experienced** an unexpected technical outage."
* **Role A:** "I see. Had the team tested the update on the staging server before launching?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. They **had already verified** all security patches, but the hardware failure occurred right as the update started."

**Scenario 2: Reviewing Client Meeting Arrangements**
* **Context:** Two corporate consultants confirming meeting details before a executive presentation.
* **Role A:** "Did you manage to send the revised proposal slides to the client before our sync?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! Before the client called us, I **had already emailed** the updated financial figures to their team lead."
* **Role A:** "Great! Had they reviewed the contract details prior to our call?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, they **hadn't checked** the second section yet, so we walked through it together."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive updates, clarify sequence of past events, or state facts politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly explaining past oversights, admitting technical delays, or sharing neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion statements when explaining unexpected past failures or reporting missing items gently. |
| **Before we started, I had already...** | A professional formula for confirming that preparatory tasks were completed prior to a deadline. |
| **By the time we noticed..., it had already...** | A clear, courteous way to explain technical or project timelines in debrief meetings. |
