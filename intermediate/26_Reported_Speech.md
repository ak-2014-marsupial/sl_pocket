# 26. Reported Speech (Непряма мова)

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reported Speech** | A way of stating what someone else said without using their exact words | Непряма мова |
| **Direct Speech** | The exact words spoken by a person, enclosed in quotation marks | Пряма мова |
| **Reporting Verb** | A verb used to report what someone said, such as "say", "tell", or "ask" | Дієслово мовлення |
| **Backshift** | The change of a tense from present/past to a past/past perfect form when converting direct speech to reported speech | Зсув часів назад |
| **Say** | A reporting verb used without a direct personal object unless preceded by "to" | Казати, говорити |
| **Tell** | A reporting verb that requires a direct personal object (who was spoken to) | Повідомляти, розповідати (комусь) |
| **The day before** | A time expression used in reported speech to replace "yesterday" | Напередодні, за день до цього |
| **The following week** | A time expression used in reported speech to replace "next week" | Наступного тижня |
| **That day** | A time expression used in reported speech to replace "today" | Того дня |
| **Then** | A time expression used in reported speech to replace "now" | Тоді |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "In Reported Speech, step one tense into the past: Present becomes Past, and Past becomes Past Perfect!"

> "Remember the golden reporting verb rule: You **say** something to someone, but you **tell** someone something!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Subject + Reporting Verb (said / told someone) + (that) + Subject + Backshifted Verb + Object`

* **Правило зсуву часів (Backshift of Tenses):**
  Якщо дієслово мовлення (*said*, *told*) вжито у минулому часі, граматичний час у непрямій мові змінюється на один крок у минуле:
  * Present Simple => Past Simple
  * Present Continuous => Past Continuous
  * Present Perfect / Past Simple => Past Perfect
  * Present Perfect Continuous / Past Continuous => Past Perfect Continuous
  * Future Simple (*will*) => *would*
  * Modal verbs: *can* => *could*, *must* => *had to*

*Example: Direct: "I don't like coffee," he said. => Reported: He said that he didn't like coffee.*

*Example: Direct: "We are going to the park," they said. \\(\rightarrow\\) Reported: They said that they were going to the park.*

*Example: Direct: "I will call you later," my friend said. \\(\rightarrow\\) Reported: My friend said that he would call me later.*

*Example: Direct: "I can help you," Peter said. \\(\rightarrow\\) Reported: Peter said that he could help me.*

*Example: Direct: "I must go now," Anna said. \\(\rightarrow\\) Reported: Anna said that she had to go then.*

* **Різниця між Say та Tell:**
  Formula: `Subject + say + (that) + Clause` / `Subject + say + to someone + (that) + Clause`
  Formula: `Subject + tell + someone + (that) + Clause`

  * **Say** використовується, коли ми переказуємо слова без обов'язкової вказівки адресата (або з прийменником *to*).
  * **Tell** обов'язково вимагає після себе вказівки на особу (додатка): *tell me*, *tell us*, *tell him*.

*Example: He said that he was late.*
*Example: He told me that he was late.*

* **Зміна покажчиків часу та місця (Time & Place Shift):**
  * *now* \\(\rightarrow\\) *then*
  * *today* \\(\rightarrow\\) *that day*
  * *yesterday* \\(\rightarrow\\) *the day before / the previous day*
  * *tomorrow* \\(\rightarrow\\) *the next day / the following day*
  * *next week* \\(\rightarrow\\) *the following week / the next week*
  * *last week* \\(\rightarrow\\) *the week before / the previous week*

### Translation Block

* Джон сказав, що він голодний. — *John said that he was hungry.*
* Вони сказали мені, що зателефонують наступного дня. — *They told me that they would call me the next day.*
* Вчитель сказав нам не запізнюватися. — *My teacher told us not to be late.*
* Бос сказав мені, що я повинен прийти до його кабінету о 15:00. — *My boss told me I had to come to his office at 3 p.m.*
* Колега сказала, що вона працює з дому того дня. — *My colleague said that she was working from home that day.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What was the last thing your friend said to you?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my friend mentioned that she had bought tickets for a concert next weekend and invited me to join her.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, he simply told me that he was going home after work because he felt a bit tired.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't remember exactly, as we only exchanged brief greetings before the meeting started.

**Q: What did someone say to you this morning?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my partner mentioned that the weather was going to be fantastic today, which really brightened my morning.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, the barista at the coffee shop just told me that my espresso would be ready in a few minutes.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid nobody spoke to me this morning, to be honest, because I left the house very early.

**Q: What did your boss or colleague tell you last time you spoke?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my manager told me that I had done an excellent job on the recent project report, which was very encouraging.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, my colleague simply informed me that the team meeting had been rescheduled to 3 p.m.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid my supervisor told me that we had to revise the budget proposal because of unexpected client requests.

**Q: What did your doctor or dentist tell you during your last visit?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my dentist told me that my teeth were in great condition and that I didn't need any treatment.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, the doctor recommended that I should drink more water and take regular breaks from my computer screen.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid my dentist mentioned that I needed to reschedule another appointment for a minor filling.

**Q: What did someone say to you on your birthday?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my best friend told me that she had organized a surprise dinner for us, which was truly amazing.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, most of my colleagues simply said that they wished me a restful and happy day.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I spent my last birthday traveling, so few people told me happy birthday in person, though I received a few messages.

**Q: What did someone tell you that surprised you?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my colleague told me that our company was expanding into new international markets next quarter, which was fantastic news.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, a neighbor mentioned that a new park was going to be built nearby next year.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid my landlord informed me that the utility prices would be increasing slightly starting next month.

**Q: What’s something your friend told you that made you happy?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my close friend told me that she had passed her professional certification exam after months of hard preparation.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, my friend mentioned that she had found a great spot for our upcoming weekend gathering.
* **Negative:** I must admit that while her news was positive—she told me she had accepted a new job—it made me a bit sad because she would have to relocate.

**Q: What did your teacher tell you last week?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my language instructor told me that my pronunciation had improved significantly over the past month.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, the teacher just reminded us that we had to complete our written assignments by Friday evening.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid my teacher told me that I needed to pay more attention to tense changes in reported speech exercises.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Executive Briefing After a Client Meeting**
* **Context:** A project lead debriefs a department manager after a high-stakes client presentation.
* **Role A:** "Could you please update me on how the negotiation went? What did the client representative say?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, she mentioned that they were generally satisfied with our proposal, but she indicated that their board needed to review the financial terms."
* **Role A:** "I see. Did she happen to say when they would make their final decision?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, she told me that they would get back to us by the following Tuesday at the latest."

**Scenario 2: Team Synchronization on Project Deadlines**
* **Context:** Two software engineers discussing updates received from team members and contractors.
* **Role A:** "Did the external QA team provide any updates regarding the security audit?"
* **Role B:** "Yes, the lead tester mentioned that they had identified a minor glitch, but he reassured us that it would be fixed by tomorrow morning."
* **Role A:** "That's a relief. Did our CTO say whether we could proceed with the deployment?"
* **Role B:** "I'm afraid she told us to hold off until the final report had been officially approved."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce news, clarify reported information gently, or present a positive update politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when conveying straightforward facts, direct statements, or routine updates without sounding blunt. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to soften bad news, report delays, or communicate administrative constraints politely. |
| **She mentioned that...** | A polite way to report informal or indirect remarks made by a third party. |
| **He indicated that...** | Used in professional contexts to report formal statements or strategic feedback. |
| **He reassured us that...** | Used when reporting statements intended to reduce anxiety or confirm safety/deadlines. |
| **Could / Would / Might** | Modal verbs used to soften questions about reported conversations or make polite requests. |
