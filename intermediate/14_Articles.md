# 14. Articles

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indefinite article** | The articles "a" and "an" used before non-specific singular countable nouns | Неозначений артикль |
| **Definite article** | The article "the" used before specific or previously mentioned nouns | Означений артикль |
| **Zero article** | The absence of an article before uncountable, plural, or generic nouns | Нульовий артикль |
| **Superlative degree** | An adjective form used to describe an object at the upper or lower limit of a quality | Найвищий ступінь порівняння |
| **Ordinal numbers** | Numbers specifying position in a series, such as first, second, or third | Порядкові числівники |
| **Mountain range** | A series of connected mountains or hill ridges (e.g., the Alps) | Гірський хребет |
| **Uncountable noun** | A noun that cannot be counted directly and has no plural form | Незлічуваний іменник |
| **Geographical names** | Proper nouns referring to places, rivers, oceans, or countries | Географічні назви |
| **Guided tour** | A short journey or excursion around a place with a professional guide | Екскурсія з гідом |
| **Unique object** | An item or entity of which there is only one in existence (e.g., the Sun) | Унікальний об'єкт |
| **Consonant sound** | A speech sound produced by obstructing airflow in the vocal tract | Приголосний звук |
| **Vowel sound** | A speech sound produced without significant constriction in the vocal tract | Голосний звук |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Use **a / an** when mentioning something for the first time, but switch to **the** once it becomes specific or known to everyone!"

> "Pronounce **the** as `/ði/` before **vowel sounds** like *the apple*, and `/ðə/` before **consonant sounds** like *the lesson*!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Indefinite Article): `a + Consonant Sound` / `an + Vowel Sound`  
Formula (Definite Article): `the + Specific Noun / Superlative / Ordinal / Unique Object / River / Ocean / Plural Country`  
Formula (Zero Article): `∅ + Plural / Uncountable Noun in General / Proper Name / Sport / Academic Subject`  

* **Неозначений артикль a / an (Indefinite Article):**  
  Уживається зі злічуваними іменниками в однині, коли мова йде про один із багатьох об'єктів, коли предмет згадується вперше (*a cute cat*), при вказуванні професій (*a pilot*), характеристик (*a good student*) або загального поняття "один" (*a pen*).
  * **a** ставиться перед **приголосним звуком**: *a cake*, *a fork*, *a woman*, *a unique opportunity* (звук `/j/`).
  * **an** ставиться перед **голосним звуком**: *an apple*, *an orange*, *an ugly dog*, *an hour* (літера `h` не вимовляється).

* **Означений артикль the (Definite Article):**  
  Уживається з іменниками (в однині та множині), коли предмет вже відомий або згадувався раніше (*The cat I saw...*), коли з контексту зрозуміло, про що йдеться (*Open the door!*), з найвищим ступенем порівняння (*the tallest man*), порядковими числівниками (*the first time*), унікальними об'єктами (*the Sun*, *the Earth*), а також із назвами океанів, морів, річок (*the Amazon River*), гірських хребтів (*the Alps*), груп островів (*the Philippines*), назвами країн у множині або зі словами *Kingdom/States/Netherlands* (*the UK*, *the USA*, *the Netherlands*).

* **Нульовий артикль ∅ (Zero Article):**  
  Артикль **НЕ вживається**, коли ми говоримо про речі взагалі у множині або незлічувані іменники (*I love flowers*, *listen to music*, *life is difficult*), з власними іменами людей, містами, більшістю країн (*Ukraine*, *Paris*), поодинокими горами (*Mount Everest*, *Hoverla*), поодинокими озерами (*Lake Baikal*, *Lake Ontario*), назвами навчальних предметів (*studies history*, *biology*), видів спорту (*play tennis*, *play football*), прийомами їжі (*for dinner*), а також у деяких фіксованих фразах (*go to bed*, *on page 15*).

*Example: I bought a new laptop yesterday, and the laptop works perfectly.*  
*Example: Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world, while the Alps are located in Europe.*  
*Example: She plays tennis every weekend and studies history at university.*  
*Example: Please turn off the light and read the text on page 15.*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Choose "a / an", "the", or Zero Article (—)

1. I bought **an** umbrella because it was raining heavily.
2. We stayed at **a** hotel near the beach.
3. **—** Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
4. She wants to become **an** engineer.
5. My brother studies **—** history at university.
6. Can you pass me **the** salt, please?
7. We saw **an** interesting documentary yesterday.
8. **The** Amazon River flows through several countries.
9. He plays **—** tennis every weekend.
10. They visited **the** Eiffel Tower during their trip to Paris.

### Exercise 2: Correct the Mistakes

1. She is a honest person. \\(\rightarrow\\) She is **an** honest person.
2. I need the information about your course. \\(\rightarrow\\) I need **—** information about your course.
3. We had a great time at the Lake Baikal. \\(\rightarrow\\) We had a great time at **—** Lake Baikal.
4. He is the doctor. \\(\rightarrow\\) He is **a** doctor.
5. I usually go to the bed at 11 p.m. \\(\rightarrow\\) I usually go to **—** bed at 11 p.m.
6. The life can be difficult sometimes. \\(\rightarrow\\) **—** Life can be difficult sometimes.
7. She studies the biology. \\(\rightarrow\\) She studies **—** biology.
8. I bought an new laptop. \\(\rightarrow\\) I bought **a** new laptop.
9. We visited a British Museum. \\(\rightarrow\\) We visited **the** British Museum.
10. They play the football every Saturday. \\(\rightarrow\\) They play **—** football every Saturday.

### Exercise 3: Complete the Paragraph (A / an, the or no article)

Last summer, I spent **a** week in **the** Netherlands. I stayed in **a** small hotel near **the** city centre. Every morning, I had breakfast and then explored **the** city. One day, I visited **a** famous museum and took **a** guided tour. **The** weather was excellent, so I spent a lot of time outside.

### Exercise 4: Multiple Choice Practice

1. Yesterday I bought **a** book and some flowers for Linda, but I lost **the** book on my way home.
2. A: I need **a** knife, can you give me one? B: You can use **the** knife on the kitchen top.
3. We wanted to watch **—** TV, but there wasn’t one in the apartment, so we just listened to **—** music.
4. Can you turn off **the** light and close **the** door, please?
5. I still need **a** pen, because **the** pen you lent me doesn’t work.
6. If you go to **the** post office, could you bring me **an** envelope?
7. A: What did you eat for **—** dinner? B: I ate **the** soup that was left from yesterday.
8. Please, read **the** text on **—** page 15.
9. Why don’t we go to **the** cinema to watch **a** film?
10. I'd like to cross **the** Atlantic on **a** cruise.

### Translation Block

* Торік я провів тиждень у Нідерландах і зупинився в маленькому готелі неподалік від центру міста. — *Last summer, I spent **a** week in **the** Netherlands and stayed in **a** small hotel near **the** city centre.*
* Чи міг би ти вимкнути світло та зачинити двері, будь ласка? — *Could you turn off **the** light and close **the** door, please?*
* Гора Еверест є найвищою вершиною світу, а річка Амазонка протікає через кілька країн. — ***—** Mount Everest is the highest peak in the world, and **the** Amazon River flows through several countries.*
* Вона працює інженером у міжнародній компанії та любить слухати музику після роботи. — *She works as **an** engineer for **an** international company and loves listening to **—** music after work.*
* Нам не потрібно було купувати квитки на виставку, тому що ми вже відвідали Британський музей. — *We didn't need to buy tickets for the exhibition because we had already visited **the** British Museum.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What is the most beautiful place you've visited, and what articles would you use to describe it?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'd say the most beautiful place I've visited is the Netherlands because the architecture in the capital is simply stunning.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on what you prefer; I really enjoyed visiting Lake Baikal for its peaceful nature and clean air.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't traveled much recently, so I haven't visited many famous landmarks or national parks.

**Q: Do you find it difficult to remember whether to use "a", "the", or no article with geographical names?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I find it quite manageable once you remember that plural countries like the UK or mountain ranges like the Alps take "the".
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I sometimes hesitate between a single mountain and a mountain range, but practice makes it much clearer over time.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I often make minor mistakes with zero articles, especially when talking about school subjects or daily meals.

**Q: How do you decide whether to use "a" or "an" when writing or speaking in English?**
* **Positive:** Actually, the rule is very straightforward — I focus on the initial sound rather than the letter, so I say "an hour" or "a unique opportunity".
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually do it automatically, though I occasionally have to double-check words starting with silent consonants.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I still mix up "a" and "an" when speaking quickly under time pressure during interviews.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Clarifying Office & Travel Information**
* **Context:** A corporate administrator and a remote specialist discussing travel and accommodation details for an upcoming conference.
* **Role A:** "Could you confirm if you booked **a hotel** near **the city centre** for our annual conference in **the Netherlands**?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! I stayed at **a small hotel** right next to **the main venue**, so **the location** is extremely convenient."
* **Role A:** "Great! Did you also manage to arrange **a guided tour** for our department team?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. I booked **an afternoon tour** around **the historic district** after **dinner**."

**Scenario 2: Reviewing Document & Email Grammar**
* **Context:** A team lead reviewing a junior developer's official report before client submission.
* **Role A:** "Would you mind checking **the document** I prepared for **the client** before I send **an email**?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, it looks very clear, but remember that **information** is uncountable, so we use no article before it."
* **Role A:** "Ah, thank you! Should I also write **the UK** or just UK when referencing our regional partner?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, you should always write **the UK** or **the United Kingdom** when using official country titles in business correspondence."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive grammar clarifications, state facts politely, or share personal travel stories. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly admitting minor grammar hesitations, sharing modest views, or giving neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion admissions of grammar mistakes, express hesitation under time pressure, or decline options gently. |
| **I'd say that...** | A humble, soft expression used to express personal opinions about travel destinations or language rules. |
| **Could you confirm if...?** | A polite, professional formula for checking logistics, hotel bookings, or document details in business settings. |
