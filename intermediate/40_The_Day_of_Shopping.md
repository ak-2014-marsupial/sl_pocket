# 40. The Day of Shopping

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Do window shopping** | Looking at goods in shop windows without intending to buy anything | Ходити по магазинах без наміру купити (просто роздивлятися) |
| **Do grocery shopping** | The activity of buying food and household supplies at a store | Ходити за продуктами |
| **Go on a shopping spree** | A short period of spending a large amount of money on shopping | Влаштувати шопінг-марафон (витратити багато грошей) |
| **Overpriced** | Too expensive; costing more than something is worth | Завищений за ціною, занадто дорогий |
| **Worth the money** | Providing good value or benefit for the amount paid | Вартий своїх грошей |
| **A good deal** | A product or service purchased at a very favorable price | Вигідна пропозиція |
| **A bargain** | Something bought for a much lower price than its actual value | Вигідна покупка зі знижкою |
| **To bargain** | To negotiate with a seller to agree on a lower price | Торгуватися |
| **Fit** | To be the correct size or shape for someone or something | Підходити за розміром (добре сидіти) |
| **Suit** | To make someone look attractive or enhance their appearance | Пасувати, личити (про стиль чи колір) |
| **Match** | To combine well with another item in color, pattern, or design | Поєднуватися, личити одне до одного |
| **Get a refund** | To receive money back after returning a purchased item | Отримати повернення грошей |
| **Can't resist (doing)** | To be unable to stop oneself from doing or buying something | Не можу втриматися (щоб не зробити щось) |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "You **fit** into the right size, a color **suits** your style, and your shoes **match** your bag!"

> "When an item is **overpriced**, try to **bargain**; if it's a **bargain**, it is definitely **worth the money**!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Size/Dimensions): `Item + fit(s) + Person / Body Part`
Formula (Style/Appearance): `Color / Clothes + suit(s) + Person`
Formula (Combination): `Item A + match(es) + Item B`
Formula (Uncontrollable Urge): `Subject + can't / couldn't resist + Verb-ing`
Formula (Value Evaluation): `Item + is / are + worth + Noun / Amount`

* **Використання дієсел Fit, Suit та Match:**
  * **Fit** вказує на розмір, форму та габарити одягу чи взуття (*These shoes fit me perfectly*).
  * **Suit** вказує на те, чи пасує стилю, кольору волосся, очей чи зовнішності людини певний одяг або зачіска (*That blue dress really suits you*).
  * **Match** описує поєднання двох предметів між собою за кольором, візерунком чи стилем (*Your handbag matches your shoes*).

* **Герундій після конструкції Can't resist:**
  Після виразу *can't resist* (не можу втриматися) дієслово завжди вживається у формі герундія з закінченням *-ing* (*I can't resist buying sweets*).

* **Конструкції оцінки вартості (Worth, Overpriced, Bargain):**
  Вираз *worth the money* означає, що якість речі повністю виправдовує її ціну, тоді як *overpriced* вказує на необґрунтовано завищену вартість.

*Example: I tried the trousers on, but they didn't fit around my waist.*
*Example: That new haircut really suits you and makes you look younger.*
*Example: Her coat matches her boots perfectly.*
*Example: She saw the chocolate cake and couldn't resist buying it.*

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Fill in the Gaps (Shopping Types)

1. We didn’t buy anything. We just did some **window shopping** after work.
2. They went on a **shopping spree** during the sales and bought a lot of clothes.
3. Let’s do some **grocery** shopping before the guests arrive. Our fridge is empty.

### Exercise 2: Fill in the Gaps (Price Evaluation)

1. This jacket is warm, stylish, and **worth** the money I paid.
2. Some designer clothes are extremely **overpriced**.

### Exercise 3: Fill in the Gaps (Deals and Bargaining)

1. These jeans were only \$50, not \$60 – such a good **deal**!
2. I bought this jacket for half price – it was a real **bargain**.
3. In some countries, it’s normal to **bargain** with the seller.

### Exercise 4: Choose Fit, Suit, or Match

1. That colour really **suits** you.
2. These shoes don’t **fit** me. They’re too small.
3. Your bag **matches** your shoes – nice look!

### Exercise 5: Fill in the Gaps (Actions and Refunds)

1. I can’t **resist** buying chocolate when I’m at the supermarket.
2. If it’s broken, you can get **a refund** at the shop.

### Exercise 6: Sentence Completion Practice 1

1. I tried the jeans on, but they didn’t **fit** around my waist.
2. She always checks if her earrings **match** her shoes before leaving the house.
3. That restaurant charged \$50 for a salad — totally **overpriced**!

### Exercise 7: Sentence Completion Practice 2

1. He wanted to return the jacket and **get a refund** because there was a hole in the sleeve.
2. The blue dress really **suits** you – it brings out your eyes!
3. They went to Milan and decided to **go on a shopping spree** without checking their credit card balance.

### Exercise 8: Sentence Completion Practice 3

1. That coat was originally \$200, and I bought it for \$40 – what a **bargain**!
2. We were in Turkey, and my dad spent twenty minutes trying to **bargain** for a carpet.
3. She saw the chocolate cake and just **couldn't resist** buying it.

### Translation Block

* Ми не купили нічого, а просто **дивилися на вітрини (doing window shopping)** після роботи. — *We didn’t buy anything, we just did some **window shopping** after work.*
* Цей піджак теплий, стильний і **вартий своїх грошей (worth the money)**. — *This jacket is warm, stylish, and **worth the money**.*
* Цей колір тобі дуже **пасує (suits)**. — *That color really **suits** you.*
* Ці туфлі мені не **підходять за розміром (don't fit)** — вони занадто малі. — *These shoes don't **fit** me — they are too small.*
* Вона не могла **втриматися від покупки (couldn't resist buying)** шоколадного торта. — *She couldn't **resist buying** the chocolate cake.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you prefer online or offline shopping? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say I much prefer online shopping because it saves time, allows me to compare prices easily, and usually offers better deals.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on what I am buying; I buy electronics online, but I prefer offline shopping for clothes so I can check if they fit.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really enjoy online shopping because returning items to get a refund can be quite time-consuming.

**Q: What is something you recently bought that was really worth the money?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I bought a high-quality winter jacket last month, and it was definitely worth the money because it is warm and waterproof.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, my new ergonomic office chair was fairly expensive, but it has made working from home much more comfortable.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't made any purchases recently that felt truly worth the money, as most items seem somewhat overpriced lately.

**Q: Do you like to bargain when you travel to foreign markets?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I find bargaining in local street markets quite entertaining because it allows you to interact with locals and get a bargain.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I only try to bargain if I know that negotiating prices is a normal part of the local culture.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I feel rather uncomfortable trying to bargain, so I prefer buying items with clear, fixed price tags.

**Q: When was the last time you went on a shopping spree? What did you buy?**
* **Positive:** Actually, the last time I went on a shopping spree was during the seasonal sales, and I bought two suits, leather shoes, and several shirts.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I rarely go on big shopping sprees, but I bought a few new outfits before my vacation last summer.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't been on a shopping spree in years, as I prefer buying items only when I genuinely need them.

**Q: What is an item you cannot resist buying when you see it on sale?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I can never resist buying good quality books or coffee beans whenever I spot a discounts promotion.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally can't resist buying fresh pastries or chocolate snacks when doing my weekly grocery shopping.
* **Negative:** I must admit I try to control my spending strictly, so I rarely buy things impulsively just because they are on sale.

**Q: Have you ever had to return an item to get a refund? What happened?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I had to return a pair of running shoes last month because they didn't fit properly, and the store issued a full refund right away.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I returned an online order once because the color didn't match the photos, though the process took a few days.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I had a negative experience trying to get a refund for a damaged item because the customer support was rather slow.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Customer Service & Return Request**
* **Context:** A customer returning a defective clothing item to a store manager.
* **Role A:** "Good afternoon. Could I please speak with the store manager regarding a recent purchase?"
* **Role B:** "Good afternoon! How can I help you today? Is there an issue with your item?"
* **Role A:** "Actually, I bought this jacket yesterday, but when I tried it on at home, I noticed the zipper was broken and it didn't **fit** properly."
* **Role B:** "I'm afraid that slipped past our quality check. Would you like to exchange it for another size, or would you prefer to **get a refund**?"
* **Role A:** "To be honest, I'd prefer to **get a refund**, as the other color doesn't really **suit** my wardrobe."
* **Role B:** "Certainly. I will process your refund immediately. Apologies for the inconvenience."

**Scenario 2: Preparing Outfits for a Corporate Photoshoot**
* **Context:** Two colleagues choosing corporate attire for an upcoming company event.
* **Role A:** "Could you help me choose an outfit for the executive photo session tomorrow?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, that dark navy suit **suits** you very well and looks extremely professional."
* **Role A:** "Thank you! Do you think these brown leather shoes **match** the belt?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, they **match** perfectly. The whole set looks high quality and was definitely **worth the money**."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive opinions, share recent purchases, or state preferences politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing candid thoughts about prices, admission of impulse buying, or neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion complaints, decline overpriced offers, or explain difficulties in getting a refund gently. |
| **It was definitely worth the money** | Used to praise a purchase or service without sounding overly enthusiastic or unprofessional. |
| **Would you prefer... or...?** | A polite, customer-oriented formula used to offer options regarding refunds or product exchanges. |
| **It brings out your...** | A polite, complimentary phrase used to explain why a specific color or style suits someone. |
