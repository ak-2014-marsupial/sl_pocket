# 47. Was / Were Supposed To & Was / Were Going To

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Was / Were supposed to** | Used to talk about things we agreed to do or other people expected us to do, but we didn’t do | Мав / мала / мали зробити (але не зробили) |
| **Was / Were going to** | Used to talk about plans or intentions in the past that did not happen | Збирався / збиралася / збиралися зробити (але план змінився) |
| **Change one's mind** | To alter one's decision, opinion, or plan after previous consideration | Змінити свою думку / рішення |
| **Get stuck in traffic** | To be delayed because of heavy vehicle congestion on the road | Застрягти в заторі |
| **Oversleep** | To sleep beyond one's intended or normal waking time | Проспати |
| **Run out of time** | To have no more time left to finish a task or action | Зазнати браку часу / не встигати |
| **Break down** | (Of a vehicle or machine) To stop functioning properly | Зламатися (про авто чи техніку) |
| **Cancel** | To decide that an arranged event or meeting will not take place | Скасувати |
| **Miss a deadline** | To fail to complete or submit something by the required time | Пропустити дедлайн / термін |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Use **was/were supposed to** when *others expected it*, but use **was/were going to** when it was *just your own plan*!"

> "When plans fail or minds change, **was/were going to** explains *what was intended*, and **was/were supposed to** highlights *what was required*!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Expectation / Obligation): `Subject + was / were + supposed to + Verb (Infinitive)`  
Formula (Intention / Personal Plan): `Subject + was / were + going to + Verb (Infinitive)`  
Formula (Negative Form): `Subject + wasn't / weren't + supposed to / going to + Verb (Infinitive)`

* **Was/Were supposed to (Зовнішнє очікування чи домовленість):**  
  Використовується для опису дій, які від нас очікували інші люди, або про які ми домовлялися чи мали зобов'язання зробити, але **НЕ зробили** через форс-мажор, забудькуватість чи зміну обставин.
* **Was/Were going to (Особистий намір чи план):**  
  Використовується для опису **власних намірів чи планів** у минулому, які не здійснилися. Ніхто цього від нас не вимагав — це був лише наш власний план, який змінився або зірвався.
* **Головна відмінність:**  
  * *Was/were supposed to* = зовнішнє очікування / домовленість / обов'язок (*their mum asked them to do it*, *my boss expected it*).  
  * *Was/were going to* = особистий намір / план (*it was just my plan, I changed my mind*).

*Example: I was supposed to book the restaurant for tonight, as my girlfriend asked me, but I forgot.*  
*Example: I was going to watch a movie last night, but I changed my mind.*  
*Example: They were supposed to clean their room, but they didn't do it.*  
*Example: David was going to play football yesterday, but he was too tired and decided not to go.*

1. They supposed to call you in the morning, but they didn’t. \\(\rightarrow\\) They **were supposed to call** you in the morning, but they didn’t.
2. He were supposed to take his medicine, but he forgot. \\(\rightarrow\\) He **was supposed to take** his medicine, but he forgot.
3. I was suppose to send you the report, but I didn’t have time. \\(\rightarrow\\) I **was supposed to send** you the report, but I didn’t have time.
4. She was supposed going to the gym, but she stayed home. \\(\rightarrow\\) She **was supposed to go** to the gym, but she stayed home.
5. We was supposed to meet our friends, but they canceled. \\(\rightarrow\\) We **were supposed to meet** our friends, but they canceled.

### Translation Block

* Вони мали прибрати свою кімнату, але не зробили цього (мама просила їх). — *They **were supposed to clean** their room, but they didn't do it.*
* Я збирався подивитися фільм учора ввечері, але передумав. — *I **was going to watch** a movie last night, but I changed my mind.*
* Я мав забронювати столик у ресторані, як просила моя дівчина, але забув. — *I **was supposed to book** the restaurant for tonight, as my girlfriend asked me, but I forgot.*
* Вони збиралися відкрити кав'ярню разом, але ідея не спрацювала. — *They **were going to open** a café together, but the idea didn’t work out.*
* Ми мали розпочати зустріч о 10:00, але бос запізнився. — *We **were supposed to start** the meeting at 10 a.m., but the boss arrived late.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What was something you were supposed to do yesterday, but couldn't?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I was supposed to submit the final quarterly report to my department manager, but I received several urgent client calls that required my immediate attention.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I was supposed to pick up some groceries after work, but I got stuck in heavy traffic and decided to go home instead.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I was supposed to complete my written assignment yesterday, but I completely ran out of energy, to be honest.

**Q: Tell me about a plan you were going to execute recently, but changed your mind about.**
* **Positive:** Actually, I was going to sign up for a new fitness membership this month, but I decided to focus on outdoor running instead.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, we were going to travel out of town over the weekend, but bad weather forced us to stay home and relax.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I was going to start a new online language course, but my current work schedule is far too demanding right now.

**Q: How do you usually handle situations when a colleague fails to do what they were supposed to do?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always approach my colleagues politely to understand if they experienced unexpected technical issues or delays before proposing a revised deadline.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I simply send a brief follow-up message to remind them about the task and check when it will be completed.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find it quite frustrating when team members miss agreed deadlines, though I try to keep my communication professional.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Missed Task & Technical Delays**
* **Context:** A project manager and a software developer discussing an unsubmitted status report during a morning sync.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! Could you update me on the client presentation slides? I noticed they aren't uploaded yet."
* **Role B:** "Actually, I **was supposed to send** the documents this morning, as requested, but my computer broke down."
* **Role A:** "I see. Were you able to resolve the issue or retrieve the files?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I **was going to work** on them from home, but I got stuck in traffic coming back from the IT support center. I'll upload them within an hour."

**Scenario 2: Project Rescheduling & Intentions**
* **Context:** Two corporate consultants reviewing why a project deliverable was delayed.
* **Role A:** "Did our team manage to finalize the budget analysis for the client yesterday?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, no. We **were supposed to finish** the testing by 4 p.m., but it took much longer than expected."
* **Role A:** "That's understandable. Were you going to notify the client manager about the shift in timeline?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. I **was going to send** him an email last night, but I decided to wait until we had precise test results this morning."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to gently explain why an obligation was missed, share positive updates, or clarify past plans politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly admitting an oversight, explaining unfulfilled plans, or sharing neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion apologies for missed expectations, report work delays, or express polite regret. |
| **I was supposed to..., but...** | A polite, standard formula for explaining why an expected task or agreement wasn't completed. |
| **I was going to..., but...** | A courteous way to explain how personal plans or intentions changed without assigning personal blame. |
| **Would it be possible to...?** | An indirect, respectful formula for asking to reschedule an obligation that wasn't completed on time. |
