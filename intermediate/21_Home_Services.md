# 21. Home Services

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Have/get a new kitchen sink installed** | To hire a plumber or specialist to fit a new sink in the kitchen | Встановити нову кухонну мийку (за допомогою майстра) |
| **Have/get a leak fixed** | To pay a professional to repair a place where liquid or gas is escaping | Полагодити протікання |
| **Have/get a carpet dry-cleaned** | To send a carpet to a professional cleaning service to remove dirt and stains | Віддати килим у хімчистку |
| **Have/get a stain removed** | To arrange for a specialist or dry cleaner to eliminate a mark from fabric or furniture | Вивести пляму |
| **Have/get the boiler serviced** | To have a certified technician inspect and maintain a heating unit | Провести обслуговування / перевірку бойлера |
| **Have/get a pipe fixed** | To employ a plumber to repair a broken or leaking water pipe | Полагодити трубу |
| **Have a heating engineer fix the heating** | To hire a qualified heating technician to repair a central heating system | Викликати інженера з опалення, щоб полагодити опалення |
| **Have a specialist assemble the furniture** | To ask a professional handyman to put together flat-pack furniture | Викликати спеціаліста, щоб зібрав меблі |
| **Get a technician to set up a new internet connection** | To persuade or hire an IT specialist to install and configure Wi-Fi/internet | Запросити техніка для налаштування нового інтернету |
| **Get a locksmith to install a new lock** | To employ a professional lock specialist to fit a new security lock on a door | Викликати слюсаря, щоб встановити новий замок |

> "Remember: you don't need to fix everything yourself—it is much safer to **have the boiler serviced** and **have a pipe fixed** by a real specialist!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Passive Causative — Service by Others): `Subject + have / get + Object (something) + Verb3 / Past Participle`  
Formula (Active Causative with Have): `Subject + have + Person (someone) + Bare Infinitive (do) + Object`  
Formula (Active Causative with Get): `Subject + get + Person (someone) + to + Infinitive (to do) + Object`  

* **Пасивна каузативна конструкція (Passive Causative — Have / Get something done):**  
  Конструкція використовується, коли дія виконується спеціалістом чи майстром для нас, а не нами особисто. Акцент робиться на виконаній роботі чи об'єкті, а не на самому виконавці.
  * *Present Simple:* *I **have the boiler serviced** every autumn.*
  * *Past Simple:* *She **had the carpet dry-cleaned** after the party.*
  * *Future / Intentions:* *We are going to **have a new sink installed** tomorrow.*
  * *Modals & Advice:* *You should **have the pipe fixed** immediately.*

* **Активна каузативна конструкція з дієсловом HAVE (Have someone do something):**  
  Уживається, коли ми зазначаємо конкретного фахівця, якому доручаємо роботу. Після дієслова *have* ставиться **інфінітив без частки to** (*bare infinitive*).
  * *We **had a heating engineer fix** the heating system.*
  * *I **will have a specialist assemble** the wardrobe.*

* **Активна каузативна конструкція з дієсловом GET (Get someone to do something):**  
  Застосовується, коли ми наймаємо, переконуємо або організовуємо людину для виконання завдання. Після дієслова *get* завжди вживається **інфінітив з часткою to**.
  * *They **got a technician to set up** the new internet connection.*
  * *He **got a locksmith to install** a new door lock.*

*Example: I need to have this wine stain removed from my armchair.*  
*Example: We had a plumber fix the leaking pipe under the bathtub.*  
*Example: Let's get a technician to set up our home Wi-Fi this Thursday.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: How often do you have the boiler serviced or call specialists for home repairs?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I make sure to have the boiler serviced every autumn before cold weather sets in, as preventative maintenance saves time and money.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the situation; I usually call a specialist only when I notice a leak or heating breakdown.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely hire home service specialists unless it's an urgent emergency, to be honest.

**Q: Do you prefer assembling new furniture yourself or having a specialist assemble it?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always prefer to have a specialist assemble the furniture because it guarantees sturdy construction and saves hours of frustration.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if it's a small shelf, I enjoy assembling it myself, but for large wardrobes, I get a specialist to do it.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't hire furniture assemblers, as I genuinely enjoy doing manual hands-on work at home.

**Q: What would you do first if you discovered a water leak under your bathroom sink?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I would turn off the main water valve immediately and get a certified plumber to fix the pipe as quickly as possible.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I'd place a bucket under the leak first and then check if I can tighten the joint before calling a plumber.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid plumbing issues cause me a lot of stress, so I would immediately call my landlord to handle the repairs.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Arranging Apartment Repairs with a Property Manager**
* **Context:** A tenant contacting a property manager regarding urgent maintenance issues in a rented apartment.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! I'm calling regarding a couple of maintenance issues in apartment 4B."
* **Role B:** "Hello! What seems to be the problem?"
* **Role A:** "Actually, the heating isn't working properly and there's a minor leak under the kitchen sink."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, we should address that right away. I will **have a heating engineer fix the heating** today and **have the pipe fixed** by tomorrow morning."

**Scenario 2: Coordinating Home Setup After Relocation**
* **Context:** A homeowner discussing move-in logistics with an interior assistant.
* **Role A:** "We've moved all the boxes into the new house. What are our top priority tasks for today?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, we need internet and security set up first."
* **Role A:** "Agreed! Should I contact the local internet service provider?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! Let's **get a technician to set up the new internet connection** this Thursday, and we can **have a specialist assemble the furniture** over the weekend."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to state facts politely, clarify maintenance arrangements, or offer practical advice. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly explaining home problems, admitting lack of technical skills, or sharing realistic timelines. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion news about home breakdowns, unexpected repair delays, or urgent costs gracefully. |
| **It's a good idea to...** | A polite, constructive formula for suggesting preventative care, such as servicing boilers or dry-cleaning carpets. |
| **We'd better have it...** | A courteous, collaborative way to propose urgent repairs or hiring professional services to family members or landlords. |
