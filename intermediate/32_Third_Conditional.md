# 32. Third Conditional

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hypothetical situation** | An imagined condition in the past that did not actually happen | Гіпотетична / уявна ситуація |
| **Regret** | A feeling of sadness or disappointment over a past action or missed chance | Жаль, розчарування / шкодувати |
| **Past consequence** | An outcome or result that would have occurred in the past under a different condition | Минулий наслідок / результат |
| **Oversleep** | To sleep beyond one's intended waking time and miss an event or transport | Проспати |
| **Pass an exam** | To achieve a successful grade on a test or assessment | Скласти іспит |
| **Miss a flight / bus** | To arrive too late to catch a plane, bus, or train | Запізнитися на рейс / автобус |
| **Lock out** | To accidentally prevent someone from entering a building or room by losing keys | Замкнути (надворі) / позбавити доступу |
| **Train hard** | To practice sports or skills intensely to achieve better results | Наполегливо тренуватися |
| **Study abroad** | To pursue education in a foreign country | Навчатися за кордоном |
| **Pay the bill** | To settle a financial charge or cost at a restaurant or shop | Оплатити рахунок |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "In Third Conditional, both the condition and the result are in the past and cannot be changed—it's pure imagination!"

> "Pronunciation shortcut: in spoken English, **would have** often sounds like **woulda** (/wʊdə/), and **could have** becomes **coulda** (/kʊdə/)!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Affirmative): `If + Subject + Past Perfect (had + V3/ed), Subject + would / could / might + have + V3/ed`  
Formula (Negative): `If + Subject + hadn't + V3/ed, Subject + wouldn't / couldn't / might not + have + V3/ed`  
Formula (Inverted Order): `Subject + would have + V3/ed + if + Subject + Past Perfect`  

* **Призначення та використання (Usage):**  
  Third Conditional (третій тип умовних речень) використовується для опису **нереальних, гіпотетичних ситуацій у минулому** та їхніх можливих наслідків. Оскільки подія вже відбулася у минулому, змінити її неможливо. Ця конструкція часто виражає жаль, критику або уявні альтернативи минулим подіям.
* **Структура речення:**  
  * **Умовна частина (If-clause):** вживається у часі *Past Perfect* (`had + V3/ed`).  
  * **Головна частина (Main clause):** містить модальне дієслово *would / could / might + have + V3/ed*.
* **Модальні дієслова у головній частині:**  
  * **Would have + V3:** описує певний, впевнений уявний результат (*I would have passed*).  
  * **Could have + V3:** вказує на минулу фізичную чи розрахункову можливість (*I could have helped you*).  
  * **Might have + V3:** виражає невпевненість або меншу ймовірність результату (*She might have passed*).
* **Особливості вимови (Pronunciation Practice):**  
  У розмовній мові *would have* часто скорочується до *would've* або вимовляється як *"woulda"* (/wʊdə/), а *could have* — до *could've* або *"coulda"* (/kʊdə/).

*Example: If I had known about the meeting, I would have been more prepared.*  
*Example: If she had studied harder, she might have passed the exam.*  
*Example: We would have avoided the traffic if we had taken the other route.*  
*Example: If I hadn't lost my wallet, I would have paid for the bill.*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Match the Two Halves of Each Sentence

1. If I had known about the meeting, — **b) I would have been more prepared.**
2. If she had studied harder, — **d) she might have passed the exam.**
3. If they had trained, — **c) they would have won the game.**
4. If we had taken a taxi, — **a) we would have come earlier.**

### Exercise 2: Fill in the Gaps (Third Conditional)

1. If I **had known** you were in town, I would have invited you to dinner.
2. If they hadn’t forgotten their tickets, they **would have seen** the concert.
3. If she had studied abroad, she **would have learned** a new language.
4. If we had left earlier, we **would not have missed** the flight.
5. If he **had been** more careful, he wouldn’t have broken the vase.
6. We would have visited more places if we **had planned** our trip better.
7. If I hadn’t overslept, I **would have caught** the bus on time.
8. I would have helped her if she **had told** me about the problem.

### Exercise 3: Correct the Mistakes

1. If I had go to the party, I would have meet her. \\(\rightarrow\\) **If I had gone to the party, I would have met her.**
2. If they had called, we might had helped. \\(\rightarrow\\) **If they had called, we might have helped.**
3. If he had took the train, he would have arrived on time. \\(\rightarrow\\) **If he had taken the train, he would have arrived on time.**
4. If she had seen the movie, she would has told me. \\(\rightarrow\\) **If she had seen the movie, she would have told me.**

### Exercise 4: Open the Brackets

1. If she **had not forgotten** her keys, she **would not have been** locked out.
2. If they **had invited** us, we **would have gone** to the wedding.
3. We **would have avoided** the traffic if we **had taken** the other route.
4. If he **had asked** me, I **would have helped** him with his project.
5. I **would have joined** you if you **had told** me about your plans.

### Exercise 5: Picture & Situation Completion (Third Conditional)

1. If I had watered my plant, it **wouldn't have died**.
2. If I had studied better for the exam, I **wouldn't have failed**.
3. If I hadn't played games at night, I **wouldn't have overslept**.
4. If I hadn't lost my wallet on the street, I **would have paid** for the bill.

### Translation Block

* Якби я знав про зустріч, я був би краще підготовлений. — *If I **had known** about the meeting, I **would have been** more prepared.*
* Ми б уникнули затору, якби поїхали іншим маршрутом. — *We **would have avoided** the traffic if we **had taken** the other route.*
* Якби вона не забула ключі, вона б не залишилася замкненою надворі. — *If she **had not forgotten** her keys, she **would not have been** locked out.*
* Якби ти попросив мене ввічливо тоді, я міг би тобі допомогти. — *If you **had asked** me nicely back then, I **could have helped** you.*
* Якби я поливав свою рослину, вона б не зів'яла. — *If I **had watered** my plant, it **wouldn't have died**.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What would you have done differently if you had known about your career path five years ago?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if I had known my career direction five years ago, I would have started learning English and specialized software much earlier.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if I had chosen a different path back then, I might have explored other industries, but I am quite content with my current experience.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't dwell on past choices, to be honest, though if I had managed my time better, I would have avoided significant stress.

**Q: How would your week have been different if you hadn't missed your main deadline last month?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if we had submitted the project proposal on time, we would have secured the new client agreement much faster.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if I hadn't missed that deadline, I would have had a much calmer weekend, but the extra review time improved our output.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid if our team had planned better, we wouldn't have run into such hectic overtime work.

**Q: If you had been able to travel anywhere last year, where would you have gone?**
* **Positive:** Actually, if I had saved enough vacation days last year, I would have traveled to Japan to explore its culture and cities.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if international flights had been more convenient, I might have visited my relatives in Europe.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I wouldn't have traveled anywhere even if I had had the chance, as I preferred focusing on my home projects.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Project Post-Mortem & Risk Analysis**
* **Context:** A project lead and a senior consultant analyzing why a previous software deployment encountered delays.
* **Role A:** "Could we review why our software deployment encountered unexpected downtime last Friday?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, if our team **had run** complete system diagnostics on Thursday, we **would have identified** the server glitch earlier."
* **Role A:** "I agree. Would extra preparation have prevented the client complaint?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. If we **had notified** the client in advance about potential maintenance, they **wouldn't have been** surprised by the delay."

**Scenario 2: Reflecting on Strategic Business Opportunities**
* **Context:** Two department managers reflecting on an overseas market expansion opportunity from last quarter.
* **Role A:** "Do you think our company made the right decision regarding the regional trade exhibition last month?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, if we **had participated** in the exhibition, we **could have met** dozens of potential international distributors."
* **Role A:** "Why did we decide not to attend back then?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, our budget was quite tight. However, if we **had allocated** funds earlier, we **would have expanded** our market reach."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive reflections on past outcomes, share lessons learned, or clarify past details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly admitting past mistakes, expressing neutral views on past regrets, or sharing realistic feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion admissions of poor planning, missed past opportunities, or unhelpful decisions gently. |
| **If we had known..., we would have...** | A constructive, professional formula for evaluating past project mistakes without placing personal blame. |
| **If we had planned better, we could have...** | A polite way to analyze strategic oversights in business post-mortems and project reviews. |
