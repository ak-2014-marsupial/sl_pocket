# 04. Work Reality Check

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hit snooze** | To press a button on an alarm clock to delay the alarm for a few extra minutes | Відкласти будильник, натиснути "повтор сигналу" |
| **Rush (to)** | To move or go somewhere very quickly due to lack of time | Поспішати, бігти (кудись) |
| **Urgent email** | A very important message that requires immediate attention or a quick response | Терміновий електронний лист |
| **To the point** | Direct and focused only on the essential information; concise and clear | По суті, коротко й чітко |
| **Schedule** | To arrange or plan a specific time for a meeting, call, or event | Запланувати, внести до розкладу |
| **Follow-up meeting** | A meeting conducted after an initial discussion to review progress or determine next steps | Повторна / подальша зустріч |
| **Juggle multiple tasks** | To manage, handle, or work on several responsibilities at the same time | Суміщати / виконувати декілька завдань одночасно |
| **Meet the deadline** | To complete a project, task, or assignment on or before the required time | Вкластися у дедлайн / виконати вчасно |
| **Log off** | To exit or disconnect from a computer system, application, or work account | Вийти з системи / завершити роботу |
| **End up (working late)** | To unexpectedly find oneself in a particular situation or doing something longer than planned | Зрештою (працювати допізна), опинитися в ситуації |
| **Push (something) to another day** | To postpone or move a meeting or discussion to a later date | Перенести (щось) на інший день |
| **Put a call on the calendar** | To record and schedule a phone call or online meeting in a digital calendar | Запланувати дзвінок у календарі |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "When you **hit snooze** and have to **rush to work**, keep your messages **to the point** so you can **meet the deadline** and **log off** on time!"

> "If you struggle to **juggle multiple tasks**, don't stress over every **urgent email**—just **schedule a follow-up meeting** or **push it to another day**."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Subject + Work-Related Verb / Phrasal Verb + Object`
Formula (Paraphrasing): `Direct Sentence \rightarrow Concise Business Collocation`

* **Використання ділових фраз та фразових дієсел (Workplace Collocations & Phrasal Verbs):**
  У професійній англійській мові замість описових або розмовних виразів вживаються усталені ділові фрази та фразові дієслова (*log off, end up, push to, hit snooze, meet the deadline*). Вони дозволяють чітко, природно та ефективно описувати щоденні робочі процеси, часові рамки та взаємодію з колегами.

* **Лаконічність та парафраз у робочій комунікації (Business Paraphrasing):**
  Ефективна ділова комунікація вимагає спрощення довгих конструкцій. Вміння швидко перефразовувати загальні фрази у точні корпоративні терміни (*"I delayed my alarm" \rightarrow "I hit snooze"*, *"His presentation was short and clear" \rightarrow "His presentation was to the point"*) покращує сприйняття вашої мови на рівні Intermediate.

*Example: She rushed to the office because her meeting started in five minutes.*
*Example: I couldn't relax during lunch because I kept getting urgent emails.*
*Example: We didn't have enough time, so we pushed the discussion to another day.*
*Example: At 6 p.m., I logged off and went for a walk.*
*Example: I only wanted to check one email, but I ended up working late.*

### Translation Block

* Я відклав будильник тричі цього ранку і зрештою спізнився на роботу. — *I **hit snooze** three times this morning and **ended up** arriving late for work.*
* Нам потрібно запланувати повторну зустріч на наступний вівторок. — *We need to **schedule** a **follow-up meeting** for next Tuesday.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you usually start work early or late?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I prefer starting my work day fairly early around 8 a.m. because it allows me to focus on key tasks before urgent messages start coming in.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my schedule, but I usually log on around 9 a.m. and adjust my working hours accordingly.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I often start work quite late because I tend to hit snooze several times in the morning, to be honest.

**Q: How often do you hit snooze in the morning?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I almost never hit snooze because I prefer getting up immediately to have a calm morning routine.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually hit snooze once or twice on Mondays, but during the rest of the week, I get up on time.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I hit snooze regularly three or four times every morning, which usually leaves me rushing to work.

**Q: Do you often have to rush to work or meetings?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I manage my daily calendar very carefully, so I rarely find myself rushing to meetings or appointments.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally have to rush if an earlier discussion runs over time, but generally, I stay on schedule.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I end up rushing to work almost every day because traffic is unpredictable and my schedule is quite tight.

**Q: How do you handle urgent emails during your workday?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I filter urgent emails immediately and keep my responses short and to the point so I can resolve issues quickly.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I check my inbox every few hours and address urgent requests first before returning to routine tasks.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I get overwhelmed when dealing with multiple urgent emails at once, which often delays my main project work.

**Q: Is it easy or difficult for you to juggle multiple tasks at once?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I find juggling multiple tasks quite manageable as long as I prioritize my workload using a digital calendar.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I can juggle two or three routine tasks at the same time, but I prefer focusing on one complex project when possible.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find it extremely difficult to juggle multiple responsibilities simultaneously without feeling drained.

**Q: What do you do when you realize you might not meet a deadline?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I inform my team lead immediately, explain the situation clearly, and propose a follow-up meeting to adjust the schedule.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if I run behind schedule, I usually work an extra hour or two to ensure I meet the required deadline.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I tend to feel quite anxious when deadlines approach, though I always try my best to complete the work.

**Q: What time do you usually log off at the end of the working day?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I make it a point to log off around 6 p.m. every evening so I can maintain a healthy work-life balance.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, my log-off time varies depending on current projects, ranging between 6:30 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I frequently end up working late and logging off after 8 p.m. because of unexpected late-afternoon tasks.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Managing Workload and Pushing Meetings**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing a busy schedule and an upcoming discussion during a demanding sprint.
* **Role A:** "Could we have a brief sync regarding the project updates this afternoon?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I'm currently juggling multiple tasks to meet the 5 p.m. deadline, so I'm a bit behind schedule."
* **Role A:** "I completely understand. Would it be possible to push our discussion to another day?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, that would be very helpful. Let's put a call on the calendar for Thursday morning instead."

**Scenario 2: Follow-Up and Urgent Request Handling**
* **Context:** A project lead checking in with a team member about client communications.
* **Role A:** "Did you have a chance to respond to the urgent email from the client this morning?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. I sent them a brief reply that was directly to the point and scheduled a follow-up meeting for next week."
* **Role A:** "Great job! Please make sure to log off on time today after you finish the remaining reports."
* **Role B:** "Thank you! I should be able to finish everything by 6 p.m."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive updates, confirm completed actions, or gently adjust proposed plans. |
| **To be honest** | Used when admitting workload pressure, explaining delays, or expressing realistic personal limits gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion news about missed deadlines, tight schedules, or needing to postpone a meeting. |
| **Would it be possible to...** | An indirect, respectful formula for asking to postpone discussions or adjust project timelines. |
| **Let's put a call on the calendar** | A professional, proactive phrase used to suggest scheduling a specific time for an upcoming meeting. |
| **To keep things to the point** | Used when setting an expectation for brief, concise, and focused communication in emails or meetings. |
