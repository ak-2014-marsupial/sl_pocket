# 09. Food Experiences

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Street food** | Ready-to-eat food or drinks sold by a vendor in a street or other public place | Вулична їжа |
| **Skewers** | Long wooden or metal pins used to hold pieces of food together during grilling | Шампури, шпажки (для шашлику) |
| **Exotic fruits** | Fruits originating from tropical or distant countries, such as mangoes or dragon fruit | Екзотичні фрукти |
| **Fermented food** | Food or drink produced through controlled microbial growth and enzymatic conversion | Ферментована їжа / продукти |
| **Donut** | A small fried ring-shaped cake of sweetened dough, often iced or glazed | Пончик, донат |
| **Chew** | To crush and grind food in the mouth with the teeth before swallowing | Жувати, пережовувати |
| **Bite** | To cut into or pierce food using the teeth | Відкушувати, кусати |
| **Sip** | To drink a liquid slowly by taking very small mouthfuls | Пити дрібними ковтками |
| **Swallow** | To cause food or drink to pass down the throat into the stomach | Ковтати |
| **Lick** | To pass the tongue over the surface of food, such as ice cream | Лизати, облизувати |
| **Taste** | To sample or test the flavor of food or drink in the mouth | Куштувати, пробувати на смак |
| **Spicy** | Flavored with or containing strong spices that produce a burning sensation | Гострий, пікантний |
| **Sour** | Having an acid taste resembling lemon or vinegar | Кислий |
| **Crispy** | Pleasantly thin, dry, and easily broken with a crunch | Хрусткий |
| **Juicy** | Full of juice, moisture, and rich flavor | Соковитий |
| **Stinky** | Having a strong, pungent, or unpleasant smell | Смердючий, з різким запахом |
| **Delicious** | Highly pleasant to the taste or smell | Смачний, вишуканий |

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Subject + Sense Verb (look / smell / taste / feel / sound) + Adjective`
Formula (with Noun): `Subject + Sense Verb + like + Noun`

* **Дієслова чуттєвого сприйняття (Verbs of Sense):**
  Дієслова *look, smell, taste, feel, sound* використовуються для опису вражень, відчуттів та зовнішніх характеристик предметів чи їжі.
* **Прикметник замість прислівника:**
  У англійській мові після дієслів чуттєвого сприйняття вживається **прикметник** (а не прислівник!).
* **Вживання з прийменником like:**
  Якщо після дієслова сприйняття іде іменник, обов'язково додається прийменник *like*.

*Example: The soup smells delicious and tastes spicy.*
*Example: Lemon juice tastes very sour.*
*Example: That street food looks like chicken skewers.*
*Example: The fermented cheese smells stinky, but it tastes surprisingly good.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Finish the sentence: I usually eat donuts when...**
* **Positive:** Actually, I usually eat donuts when I want to treat myself during a relaxed weekend breakfast with close friends.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I usually eat donuts when a colleague brings a sweet box to the office for a team celebration.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely eat donuts, to be honest, because I try to avoid sweet processed foods in my daily diet.

**Q: Finish the sentence: One exotic fruit I would like to try is...**
* **Positive:** Actually, one exotic fruit I would really love to try is fresh dragon fruit while traveling through Southeast Asia.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I would be interested in tasting fresh papaya or passion fruit if I find them at a local market.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really enjoy exotic fruits, as I generally prefer sticking to local apples and berries.

**Q: How would you order food at a restaurant with friends when the waiter asks for your order?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d like to order the grilled chicken skewers because they look juicy and smell delicious!
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I’m not particularly hungry, so can I just have a fresh green salad and sparkling water?
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't decided yet; could you give us a few more minutes to review the menu, please?

**Q: What would you say when visiting a street food festival in another country?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I decided to try the local spicy skewers, and I was pleasantly surprised because they tasted amazing!
* **Neutral:** To be honest, the street food market looked very crowded, but I managed to sip some refreshing lemon tea.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I was a bit hesitant to try the fermented street food because it smelled quite stinky, to be honest.

**Q: What would you say when meeting a friend at a café for coffee and dessert?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d like to try their signature chocolate donut because the desserts here look crispy and delicious!
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I think I'll just sip a hot cappuccino while we catch up on our news.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I can't stay very long, but I would love to order a quick espresso before I leave.

**Q: What advice would you give if your friend wants you to try an unusual dish?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I think you should definitely taste it because exploring new culinary cultures is always exciting!
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I would try a small bite first to see if the flavor appeals to you before ordering a full meal.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I wouldn't recommend trying stinky fermented dishes unless you are comfortable with strong flavors.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Catering Selection for a Business Reception**
* **Context:** An event organizer and a catering coordinator reviewing menu options for an international client networking evening.
* **Role A:** "Could we review the finger food options for tomorrow's business reception? We need choices that look presentation-ready."
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest serving mini chicken **skewers** and **crispy** bruschetta because they look professional and taste **delicious**."
* **Role A:** "That sounds great! What about beverage choices for guests during the opening discussion?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, offering fresh fruit juices and iced **lemon tea** for guests to **sip** quietly would be ideal."

**Scenario 2: Team Lunch at an Ethnic Restaurant**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing menu recommendations during a company team lunch.
* **Role A:** "Have you decided what to order from the lunch menu? The chef's specials look quite interesting."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I'm thinking of ordering the grilled salmon. I heard it tastes very fresh and **juicy**."
* **Role A:** "Actually, if you like intense flavors, their **spicy** curry is fantastic, though it smells a bit strong."
* **Role B:** "I'm afraid I can't eat very **spicy** dishes, so I'll stick to the salmon, but thanks for the recommendation!"

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive dining choices, recommend dishes, or gently correct ordering details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing candid taste preferences, mild dietary restrictions, or simple orders gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to decline unusual food offers, express hesitation regarding strong flavors, or ask for extra time politely. |
| **I'd like to try...** | A polite, professional formula for ordering food in restaurants or expressing culinary preferences. |
| **Can I have...** | An indirect, courteous way to request menu items, drinks, or extra napkins from restaurant staff. |
| **I would / wouldn't recommend...** | Used when giving professional advice or friendly opinions regarding restaurant dishes and street food. |
