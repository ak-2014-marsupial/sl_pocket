# 37. Is a Hobby a Must?

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Take (acting/cooking...) courses** | To enroll in and participate in educational classes to learn new creative or practical skills | Проходити курси (акторської майстерності/кулінарії…) |
| **Grow a balcony garden** | To cultivate plants, flowers, or herbs on a balcony space | Вирощувати сад на балконі |
| **Attend exhibitions** | To visit public displays of art, photography, or historical items | Відвідувати виставки |
| **Spend quality time with** | To dedicate meaningful, focused, and enjoyable time to friends or family | Проводити якісний час з |
| **Volunteer at events** | To offer one's time and services freely to assist at charitable or public occasions | Бути волонтером на заходах |
| **Go on a casual date with** | To go on an informal, relaxed romantic or friendly outing | Піти на невимушене побачення з |
| **Try out street food places** | To sample local meals and snacks from outdoor food stalls or courts | Пробувати їжу на вуличних фудкортах |
| **Take part in open mic nights** | To perform or participate in live events open to amateur musicians or comedians | Брати участь у вечорах відкритого мікрофона |
| **Go to live music gigs** | To attend live musical performances or small concerts in person | Ходити на живі музичні виступи / гіги |
| **Redecorate your space** | To change the interior design, colors, or arrangement of one's room or home | Оновити інтер'єр свого простору |
| **Do indoor rock climbing** | To practice climbing artificial rock walls inside a specialized facility | Займатися скелелазінням у приміщенні |
| **Host a game night with friends** | To organize an evening where friends gather to play board or party games | Влаштувати вечір ігор з друзями |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Whether you choose to **grow a balcony garden** or **do indoor rock climbing**, having a hobby is the best way to **spend quality time** with yourself and others!"

> "Be brave enough to **take part in open mic nights** and **try out street food places** whenever you need a fresh boost of inspiration!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Gerund as Subject): `Gerund (Verb-ing) + Verb (is / helps) + Object / Complement`
Formula (Intentions & Decisions): `Subject + decide / want / plan + to + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula (Habits & Frequency): `Whenever + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + Present Simple`

* **Герундій (Gerund - V-ing) у ролі підмета:**
  Якщо назва діяльності чи хобі виступає головним членом речення (підметом), дієслово вживається у формі герундія з закінченням *-ing* (*Spending quality time with friends helps you relax*).
* **Інфінітив з часткою to після дієслів прийняття рішень:**
  Після дієслів наміру та вибору (*decide, want, plan, learn*) вживається інфінітив з часткою *to* (*I decided to take acting courses*).
* **Конструкції з підрядними реченнями часу (Whenever):**
  Сполучник *whenever* (щоразу коли) використовується для опису регулярних дій та хобі у теперішньому часі (*Whenever I attend exhibitions, I get motivated*).

*Example: Spending quality time with your friends can help you relax after a stressful week.*
*Example: I decided to take acting courses because I've always wanted to feel more confident on stage.*
*Example: Whenever we travel, we try out street food places to taste something authentic.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you think having a hobby is essential for maintaining work-life balance?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say having a hobby is absolute must because activities like doing indoor rock climbing or growing a balcony garden give you a complete mental break from daily work stress.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the person; some people enjoy going to live music gigs on weekends, while others prefer simply resting at home.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't have much time for structured hobbies right now, to be honest, though I try to spend quality time with my family whenever possible.

**Q: What is an activity or creative pursuit you would love to try out first?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I would love to take acting or cooking courses because learning new practical skills always boosts my confidence.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I'm quite keen on redecorating my space or visiting local street food places during my next vacation.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid active outdoor hobbies don't appeal to me very much, as I prefer quiet indoor activities like reading.

**Q: Have you ever volunteered at events or taken part in open mic nights?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I volunteered at a charity festival last summer, and it turned out to be an inspiring experience that allowed me to meet amazing people.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I enjoy attending open mic nights as an audience member, but I haven't performed on stage myself yet.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I would feel far too nervous to perform at an open mic night, to be honest.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discussing Work-Life Balance in the Office**
* **Context:** Two team members chatting during a coffee break about weekend plans and stress recovery.
* **Role A:** "How do you usually unwind after a busy project sprint? You always seem remarkably energized."
* **Role B:** "Actually, I make sure to **do indoor rock climbing** twice a week and **host a game night with friends** on Fridays."
* **Role A:** "That sounds like a great routine! Does it help you disconnect from work completely?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. Engaging in physical challenges **lets me stop overthinking** complex project deadlines."

**Scenario 2: Planning a Team Social Outing**
* **Context:** A team lead and a project coordinator organizing an informal team-building activity.
* **Role A:** "Could we brainstorm ideas for our upcoming department social outing next Thursday?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest we **try out street food places** downtown and then **go to live music gigs**."
* **Role A:** "That sounds fantastic! Will that format give everyone a chance to chat informally?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. A casual evening will allow colleagues across different teams to **spend quality time with** each other."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive hobby recommendations, suggest team activities, or offer constructive ideas politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing candid personal preferences, admitting lack of free time, or sharing neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion admissions of stage fright, decline active invitations, or acknowledge time limitations gently. |
| **I'd say that...** | A humble, soft expression used to share personal views on work-life balance and creative hobbies. |
| **It gives you a chance to...** | Used in professional and social planning to highlight the emotional and relational benefits of a shared activity. |
