# 43. Childhood Then and Now

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Back then** | At that time in the past | Тоді, у ті часи |
| **In my childhood** | During the period of time when one was a child | У моєму дитинстві |
| **Behave well** | To act in a polite, proper, and acceptable manner | Добре поводитися |
| **Misbehave** | To act badly, disobediently, or improperly | Погано поводитися |
| **Get in trouble** | To face punishment or reprimand for doing something wrong | Мати неприємності / втрапити в халепу |
| **Make a mess** | To create disorder, untidiness, or dirty conditions | Влаштовувати безлад / смітити |
| **Climb trees** | To ascend or go up trees for play or recreation | Лазити по деревах |
| **Have sleepovers** | To stay overnight at a friend's house for fun | Ночувати в друзів |
| **Play hide-and-seek** | To play a children's game where one player hides and others look for them | Грати в хованки |
| **Collect stickers / cards / toys** | To accumulate items such as stickers, cards, or toys as a hobby | Колекціонувати наліпки / картки / іграшки |
| **Watch cartoons** | To view animated television shows or films | Дивитися мультфільми |
| **Get good / bad grades** | To receive high or low marks for academic work at school | Отримувати хороші / погані оцінки |
| **Be the teacher's pet** | To be the favorite student of a teacher | Бути улюбленцем учителя |
| **Change schools** | To move from one educational institution to another | Змінювати школу |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "In my **childhood**, we loved to **climb trees** and **play hide-and-seek**, even if we got **in trouble** for **making a mess**!"

> "Good students **behave well** and **get good grades**, but even the **teacher's pet** loved to **have sleepovers** on weekends."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula: `Subject + Verb (Past Simple / used to + Infinitive) + Past Time Marker`
Formula (Negative): `Subject + didn't use to / didn't + Verb (Infinitive)`

* **Вираження минулих звичок та дитячих спогадів (Describing Childhood Habits):**
  Для опису дитячих звичок, ігор та стану в минулому використовуються Past Simple та граматична конструкція *used to + інфінітив*.
* **Часові маркери дитинства (Past Time Markers):**
  Часові маркери *back then* (тоді, у ті часи) та *in my childhood* (у моєму дитинстві) вказують на конкретний період у минулому й зазвичай ставляться на початку або наприкінці речення.

*Example: In my childhood, I used to climb trees with my friends every weekend.*
*Example: Back then, we didn't have smartphones, so we played hide-and-seek outdoors.*
*Example: When I was ten, my family had to change schools because of my dad's job.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What were you like at school when you were a child?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say I was quite a diligent student who always tried to behave well and get good grades in most subjects.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I was a fairly average student; I behaved well in class, though I wasn't exactly the teacher's pet.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I used to misbehave quite often, to be honest, which frequently got me into trouble with my teachers.

**Q: What were your favorite games to play as a child? Why did you enjoy them?**
* **Positive:** Actually, my absolute favorite activity in my childhood was climbing trees and playing hide-and-seek with my neighbors all afternoon.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I mostly enjoyed sitting indoors on Saturday mornings to watch cartoons and collect stickers with my sister.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I didn't play many active outdoor games as a child because my family had to change schools and relocate frequently.

**Q: Describe a moment when you got in trouble as a child. What happened?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I remember getting into trouble after making a huge mess in the backyard while trying to build a wooden treehouse.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I accidentally broke a decorative vase back then while playing ball indoors, which made my parents quite upset.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely got in trouble as a child because I was naturally shy and always tried to be the teacher's pet.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Icebreaker Discussion at a Corporate Retreat**
* **Context:** Two colleagues sharing personal childhood stories during an informal corporate icebreaker event.
* **Role A:** "Could you tell me a little bit about what you were like in your childhood? Were you an active kid?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! Back then, I used to **climb trees** and **play hide-and-seek** until sunset every weekend."
* **Role A:** "That sounds fun! Were you always well-behaved at school as well?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I usually tried to **behave well** and **get good grades**, though I occasionally **got in trouble** for talking during class."

**Scenario 2: Discussing Adaptability & Career Background**
* **Context:** A senior manager and a junior colleague discussing adaptability to changing corporate environments.
* **Role A:** "Do you find it difficult when our company reorganizes departments or updates software systems?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, not really. I had to **change schools** twice during my childhood, so I'm quite used to adapting to new environments."
* **Role A:** "That's a great trait! Were you always organized as a student?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I wasn't the **teacher's pet**, but I learned early on not to **make a mess** of my schedule."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce pleasant childhood memories, clarify past details, or share personal stories politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when admitting past misbehavior, sharing candid school memories, or expressing neutral views gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to soften negative answers about school experiences, strict rules, or frequent relocations. |
| **Back then, I used to...** | A polite, natural formula for sharing personal childhood habits and nostalgic anecdotes. |
| **I'd say that...** | Used to summarize past personal characteristics or school behavior modestly. |
