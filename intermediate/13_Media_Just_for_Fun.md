# 13. Media: Just for Fun or More?

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Binge-watch** | To watch many episodes of a TV series or show one after another in a short time | Дивитися серіали/шоу запоєм (попільним переглядом) |
| **A must-watch** | A movie, TV show, or series that is so good that everyone should see it | Обов'язковий до перегляду |
| **Twist ending** | An unexpected, surprising ending to a story, movie, or book | Несподівана кінцівка |
| **Cliffhanger** | An exciting or tense ending to an episode or section that leaves you in suspense | Кульмінаційний момент (кліфгенгер) |
| **Page-turner** | A book that is so exciting or gripping that you cannot stop reading it | Книга, що читається на одному подиху |
| **A quick read** | A short book or text that is simple and easy to finish quickly | Коротка книга (що легко читається) |
| **Self-help book** | A book that gives advice and guidance for personal development, confidence, or life | Книга з саморозвитку |
| **Listen on repeat** | To play a song, album, or playlist again and again continuously | Слухати на повторі |
| **A catchy tune** | A pleasant song or melody that is easy to remember and sing | Мелодія, що легко запам'ятовується |
| **Feel-good music** | Uplifting or cheerful music that makes you feel happy and improves your mood | Музика, що піднімає настрій |
| **Background music** | Quiet music played softly in the background while people do other activities | Фонова музика |
| **Go viral** | To quickly become extremely popular and widely shared across social media or the internet | Стати вірусним (швидко набути популярності) |
| **Share a link / post / TikTok** | To send a web link, post, or short video to someone online | Поділитися посиланням / постом / Тік-Током |
| **Rewind** | To move backwards in an audio or video track to re-watch or re-listen to a section | Перемотати назад |
| **Fast-forward** | To move forwards through a video or audio track to skip unwanted parts | Перемотати вперед |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "When a book is a true **page-turner**, you won't stop until the **twist ending**; when a show is a **must-watch**, you'll **binge-watch** every single episode!"

> "Start your morning with a **catchy tune** and **feel-good music**, and if a video is hilarious enough, it will **go viral** in hours!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Infinitive / Gerund Preferences): `Subject + love / prefer + to + binge-watch / listen on repeat + Noun`  
Formula (Action Sequence with Media): `Subject + rewind / fast-forward + (through) + Noun`  
Formula (Descriptive Noun Phrases): `Subject + is + a page-turner / a quick read / a must-watch`  

* **Колокації та структура речень у сфері медіа (Media Collocations):**  
  Дієслова перегляду та прослуховування поєднуються з відповідними іменниками або прийменниковими фразами (*binge-watch a TV show*, *listen on repeat*, *go viral on social media*).
* **Прикметники та складені іменники для опису контенту (Media Descriptors):**  
  Іменникові сполучення *must-watch*, *page-turner*, *quick read*, *twist ending*, та *cliffhanger* вживаються з неозначеним артиклем **a/an** у ролі іменникової частини присудка (*This film is a must-watch*).
* **Фразові дієслова навігації аудіо/відео (Media Control Verbs):**  
  * **Rewind** — перекручувати назад (*rewind to the beginning*).  
  * **Fast-forward** — перекручувати уперед (*fast-forward through the ads*).  

*Example: This crime novel is a real page-turner — I couldn't put it down.*  
*Example: When I feel sad, I put on some feel-good music to cheer myself up.*  
*Example: The funny cat video went viral and got a million views in one day.*  
*Example: I wanted to skip the ads, so I fast-forwarded through the first two minutes.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What is a must-watch movie or series with a great cliffhanger that made you want to keep watching?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say the recent thriller series on Netflix was an absolute must-watch because every episode ended with a massive cliffhanger that kept me up all night.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I don't watch series very often, but I recently saw a detective film with a clever cliffhanger between seasons that was quite engaging.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't enjoy shows with constant cliffhangers, to be honest, as I prefer completed storylines without excessive drama.

**Q: What kind of music do you like to listen on repeat? Does it have a catchy tune or is it mostly feel-good music?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I love putting feel-good music on repeat while working because a catchy tune helps me stay energized and focused throughout the day.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my workload; I usually play soft background music when studying, but switch to catchy tunes when exercising.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely listen to songs on repeat, as repeating the same melody too often makes it lose its charm for me.

**Q: Have you ever binge-watched a TV show or a film that had a surprising twist ending? Tell us about it.**
* **Positive:** Actually, I once binge-watched a mystery drama in two days, and the twist ending was so unexpected that I had to rewind the final scene to absorb it.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I've binge-watched a few popular shows, though the twist endings were somewhat predictable if you paid close attention.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't have enough spare time to binge-watch entire shows, so I prefer reading a quick read before bed instead.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Casual Team Icebreaker & Media Recommendations**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing entertainment recommendations during a morning virtual coffee break.
* **Role A:** "Good morning! Did you do anything interesting over the weekend to unwind?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! I managed to **binge-watch** a new documentary series that turned out to be a total **must-watch**."
* **Role A:** "That sounds great! Did it have an engaging storyline?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, every single episode ended on a dramatic **cliffhanger**, so I ended up staying up much later than planned!"

**Scenario 2: Sharing Industry News & Viral Content**
* **Context:** A digital marketing specialist and a team lead discussing a successful online campaign.
* **Role A:** "Could you check why our brand's short video received so many website visits this morning?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, our promotional clip **went viral** on social media after a tech influencer decided to **share a link** to it."
* **Role A:** "That's fantastic news! What made the video so popular?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, it features **feel-good music** and **a catchy tune** that instantly grabs viewers' attention."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive entertainment recommendations, give enthusiastic updates, or correct assumptions politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when admitting candidly that you don't watch popular shows, prefer quiet background music, or lack time. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion negative answers when declining media trends, disliking cliffhangers, or stating time limits gently. |
| **I'd say that...** | A humble, soft expression used to summarize personal tastes in books, movies, or music modest. |
| **It depends on...** | Used when explaining how your choice of background music or reading habits changes based on mood or workload. |
