# 07. Healthy Eating Habits

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oatmeal** | A hot cereal made from rolled or ground oats boiled in water or milk | Вівсянка |
| **Homemade snacks** | Light food prepared at home rather than bought pre-packaged | Домашні перекуси |
| **Nuts** | Hard-shelled dry fruits or seeds rich in healthy fats and proteins | Горіхи |
| **Leafy greens** | Vegetables with edible leaves, such as lettuce, kale, and spinach | Листова зелень |
| **Beans** | Edible seeds of various leguminous plants high in fiber and plant protein | Бобові / квасоля |
| **Lean meat** | Meat with a low fat content, such as chicken breast or turkey | Нежирне м’ясо |
| **Healthy fats** | Beneficial fats found in avocado, nuts, fish, and olive oil | Корисні жири |
| **Whole-grain toast** | A slice of whole-wheat bread browned by heat, rich in dietary fiber | Цільнозерновий тост |
| **Spinach** | A dark green leafy vegetable rich in iron and essential vitamins | Шпинат |
| **Veggie sticks** | Sliced raw vegetables like carrots or cucumbers served as a healthy snack | Овочеві палички |
| **Processed food** | Food altered from its natural state, often containing added salt, sugar, or preservatives | Оброблена їжа / ультраоброблені продукти |
| **Carbs** | Short for carbohydrates; nutrients that provide primary energy to the body | Вуглеводи |
| **Herbal tea** | An infusion of dried herbs, flowers, or spices served hot or cold | Трав'яний чай |
| **Sauce** | A liquid or semi-solid condiment served with food to add flavor or moisture | Соус |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Choose **leafy greens** and **lean meat** to keep your energy high, and swap **processed food** for **homemade snacks**!"

> "Fuel your morning with **oatmeal** and **healthy fats**, then relax in the evening with a warm cup of **herbal tea**."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Modal Recommendations): `Subject + should / shouldn't + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula (Preferences): `Subject + prefer + Verb+ing / Noun + to + Verb+ing / Noun`
Formula (Would Rather): `Subject + would rather + Verb (Infinitive) + than + Verb (Infinitive)`

* **Надання порад щодо здорового способу життя (Modal Advice):**
  Модальне дієслово *should* вживається для надання м'яких рекомендацій, порад щодо раціону та формування здорових звичок.
* **Вираження вподобань у харчуванні (Expressing Food Preferences):**
  * Конструкція *prefer... to...* використовується для порівняння двох іменників або дій у герундії.
  * Конструкція *would rather... than...* вживається з голим інфінітивом для вираження надання переваги одній дії над іншою у конкретній ситуації.

*Example: If you want to lose weight, you should choose lean meat instead of fatty meat.*
*Example: I prefer eating veggie sticks to eating potato chips.*
*Example: I would rather drink herbal tea in the evening than drink coffee.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Do you prefer eating oatmeal or whole-grain toast for breakfast? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say I prefer eating oatmeal in the morning because combining it with fruits and nuts provides long-lasting energy for my workday.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it usually depends on how much time I have; I go for whole-grain toast when I’m in a hurry, but I make oatmeal on weekends.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't really enjoy either option, to be honest, as I usually just have a cup of black coffee for breakfast.

**Q: Do you often add leafy greens like spinach to your meals?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I try to add fresh spinach or leafy greens to my salads and lunch bowls almost every day to maintain a balanced diet.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally buy spinach when I cook pasta or soups, but I don't eat it on a daily basis.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely consume leafy greens, as I find it quite challenging to keep them fresh in my refrigerator.

**Q: Would you rather drink herbal tea or eat veggie sticks as a snack in the evening?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I would much rather drink warm herbal tea in the evening because it helps me unwind and relax before going to sleep.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I enjoy both depending on whether I feel physically hungry or just want something soothing after dinner.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid neither option sounds particularly appealing to me late at night, as I occasionally crave sweet homemade snacks instead.

**Q: How often do you eat lean meat with healthy fats like avocado or olive oil?**
* **Positive:** Actually, preparing grilled chicken breast with avocado and a drizzle of olive oil is a core part of my weekly meal planning.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I consume lean meat two or three times a week, though I don't pay very strict attention to measuring healthy fats.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't cook lean meat very often because I follow a largely plant-based diet focused on beans and lentils.

**Q: Do you agree that food affects how we feel? Why or why not?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I completely agree that food directly influences energy levels; avoiding processed food always makes me feel significantly more active and focused.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I believe food plays a role in physical well-being, though sleep and stress levels seem equally influential to me.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't noticed a dramatic difference in my mood based solely on my diet, to be honest.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Corporate Catering & Healthy Office Snacks**
* **Context:** An office manager and a wellness coordinator planning healthy food choices for a corporate workshop.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss the catering options for next week's corporate training? We want to ensure healthy choices for our participants."
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest replacing pastries with **homemade snacks**, **veggie sticks** with hummus, and fresh **nuts**."
* **Role A:** "That sounds like a great idea. Should we also provide light lunch options like salads with **lean meat**?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, that would be ideal. Avoiding heavy **processed food** will help everyone stay attentive throughout the afternoon sessions."

**Scenario 2: Discussing Dietary Habits During Business Travel**
* **Context:** Two colleagues reviewing dining preferences while attending an international business conference.
* **Role A:** "Would you like to join me for dinner at the hotel restaurant tonight? They offer a varied healthy menu."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I'd love to. I'm trying to stick to lighter meals with **healthy fats** and **leafy greens** after sitting in conference rooms all day."
* **Role A:** "Actually, they have a fantastic dish with grilled salmon, **spinach**, and **whole-grain toast**."
* **Role B:** "That sounds perfect! I'd much rather order that than heavy pasta with rich **sauce**."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to introduce positive dietary habits, suggest healthy catering options, or share personal achievements politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing honest eating preferences, admitting busy cooking routines, or sharing neutral feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion statements about unkept diet goals, declining certain foods, or acknowledging lack of time gracefully. |
| **I'd much rather... than...** | A polite, firm formula for expressing clear preferences regarding meal options or healthy lifestyle choices. |
| **I try to make it a habit to...** | Used to describe personal wellness routines without sounding boastful or overly strict. |
| **Would you prefer... or...?** | An indirect, respectful way to ask colleagues or guests about their dietary preferences or meeting catering. |
