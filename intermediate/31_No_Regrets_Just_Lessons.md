# 31. No Regrets, Just Lessons

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Burnout** | A state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress | Вигорання |
| **Anxiety** | A feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about an uncertain outcome | Тривога |
| **To regret (a regret)** | To feel sad, repentant, or disappointed over a past action or missed chance | Шкодувати / шкодування |
| **Opportunity** | A set of circumstances that makes it possible to do something beneficial | Можливість |
| **Turn down a job offer** | To refuse or decline a proposal for employment | Відхилити пропозицію про роботу |
| **Avoid networking opportunities** | To intentionally stay away from events where professional contacts are made | Уникати можливостей для налагодження контактів |
| **Ask for a promotion** | To formally request a higher position, rank, or salary at work | Просити про підвищення |
| **Reject a job relocation** | To decline a company's requirement or offer to move to another city or country for work | Відмовитися від переїзду по роботі (релокації) |
| **Spend quality time with** | To dedicate focused, meaningful, and uninterrupted time to family or friends | Проводити якісний час з |
| **Lose touch with someone** | To gradually stop communicating or maintaining contact with a person | Втратити зв'язок з кимось |
| **Ignore signs of stress** | To pay no attention to physical or emotional indicators of strain and fatigue | Ігнорувати ознаки стресу |
| **Express feelings** | To communicate or show one's emotional state openly to others | Виражати почуття / емоції |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Don't **ignore signs of stress** until you suffer from **burnout**; take every good **opportunity** and never **regret** making a brave choice!"

> "It's better to **ask for a promotion** or **express feelings** openly than to **lose touch with** what truly matters to you."

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Gerund after Regret): `Subject + regret + Verb-ing`  
Formula (Negative Gerund after Regret): `Subject + regret + not + Verb-ing`  
Formula (Infinitive after Opportunity): `Opportunity + to + Verb (Infinitive)`  
Formula (Phrasal Verb Patterns): `Subject + turn down / ask for + Noun`  

* **Вживання герундія після дієслова Regret (Expressing Past Regrets):**  
  Дієслово *regret* вживається з герундієм (*Verb-ing*), коли висловлюється жаль щодо дій чи рішень у минулому (*I regret turning down that offer*).  
  У заперечній формі частка *not* ставиться безпосередньо перед герундієм (*I regret not taking that internship*).
* **Конструкції з іменником Opportunity (Opportunity + Infinitive):**  
  Після іменника *opportunity* вживається інфінітив з часткою *to* для позначення мети чи можливості виконати дію (*He saw it as an opportunity to learn*).
* **Фразові дієслова у професійному контексті (Phrasal Verbs in Career):**  
  * *Turn down* означає відхилити або відмовити (*turn down a job offer*).  
  * *Ask for* вживається для звернення з проханням (*ask for a promotion*).  
  * *Lose touch with* використовується для опису втрати контактів з людьми.

*Example: I regret not taking that internship when I had the chance.*  
*Example: Public speaking gives me a lot of anxiety.*  
*Example: He saw the conference as a great opportunity to connect with others.*  
*Example: She decided to turn down the job offer because the salary was too low.*  

## 4. Written Practice

3. He didn’t want to miss the **opportunity** to speak at an international conference.

1. When I feel **burnout** coming, I usually try to take a short vacation and disconnect from work chats.
2. I really **regret** not taking the **opportunity** to study abroad during my university years.
3. People often feel **anxiety** when they face public speaking or unexpected job interviews.
4. I once **turned down a job offer** because the company culture didn't align with my values.

### Translation Block

* Я шкодую, що не скористався тією стажуванням, коли мав таку можливість. — *I **regret not taking** that internship when I had the chance.*
* Постійний тиск на роботі викликав у неї сильну тривогу. — *The constant pressure at work caused her severe **anxiety**.*
* Вона відхилила пропозицію про роботу, тому що зарплата була занадто низькою. — *She **turned down the job offer** because the salary was too low.*
* Він шкодує, що уникав можливостей для нетворкінгу на початку кар'єри. — *He regrets **avoiding networking opportunities** early in his career.*
* Деяким людям важко виражати свої почуття. — *Some people find it hard to **express their feelings**.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Have you ever regretted turning down a job offer or missing a professional opportunity?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d say I have no major regrets because even when I turned down a job offer in the past, it allowed me to find a much better position later.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I occasionally wonder what would have happened if I had taken an overseas internship, but I prefer focusing on present goals.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I really regret missing a key networking opportunity last year, as it could have accelerated my career growth.

**Q: How do you handle workplace anxiety and prevent burnout during stressful periods?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I try not to ignore signs of stress and make sure to spend quality time with my family every evening to recharge.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, managing stress depends on the project pace; I take short breaks during the day, though staying completely calm can be tricky.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I suffer from severe anxiety during tight sprint deadlines, to be honest, and I struggle to avoid burnout.

**Q: Do you find it easy to ask for a promotion when you feel you've earned it?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I feel quite confident asking for a promotion when I can present clear professional results and completed milestones.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it takes some preparation; I schedule a formal meeting with my manager rather than bringing it up informally.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find it rather intimidating to ask for a promotion, so I usually wait for performance reviews instead.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Discussing Career Advancement & Job Relocation**
* **Context:** A team member discussing career growth and relocation options with a department lead.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss my current career trajectory? I'm interested in exploring higher-level responsibilities."
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! If you feel you've earned it, this is a great time to **ask for a promotion**."
* **Role A:** "Thank you! Does the new role require a **job relocation** to the regional office?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, that option is open, but if you prefer to stay local, you can **reject a job relocation** and remain in our current team."

**Scenario 2: Addressing Workload Fatigue & Burnout Prevention**
* **Context:** Two senior managers reviewing team well-being and project deadlines during a coffee break.
* **Role A:** "Have you noticed how tired the team looks after working 12-hour days this month?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, yes. We shouldn't **ignore signs of stress** because several engineers are starting to experience **burnout**."
* **Role A:** "I agree completely. Should we adjust our sprint timeline?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, that would be ideal. Encouraging staff to disconnect and **spend quality time with** their families will improve overall productivity."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive career outcomes, offer constructive suggestions, or state facts politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly expressing work stress, acknowledging past mistakes, or sharing modest feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion admissions of anxiety, express hesitation about career decisions, or report burnout gently. |
| **If you feel you've earned it...** | A supportive, professional formula for encouraging colleagues to ask for promotions or raises. |
| **It is important to...** | A respectful, professional way to emphasize work-life balance and mental health priorities. |
