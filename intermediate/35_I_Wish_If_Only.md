# 35. I Wish / If Only

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regret** | A feeling of sadness or disappointment over a past action or present situation | Жаль, розчарування / шкодувати |
| **Annoyance** | The feeling of being irritated or impatient with someone's behavior | Роздратування |
| **Unreal situation** | An imaginary or hypothetical condition that is contrary to fact | Нереальна / уявна ситуація |
| **Fluently** | Smoothly and effortlessly, especially when speaking a language | Вільно (про мову) |
| **Litter** | To leave garbage or rubbish lying around in public places | Смітити |
| **Irresponsible** | Lacking a sense of duty, care, or obligation | Невідповідальний |
| **Concentrate on** | To direct all one's attention toward a specific activity or task | Концентруватися на |
| **Hypothetical** | Based on imagined ideas or scenarios rather than reality | Гіпотетичний, уявний |
| **Dissatisfaction** | Lack of satisfaction or pleasure with a situation or outcome | Незадоволеність |
| **Impatience** | Irritation or eagerness caused by delay or annoyance | Нетерплячість |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Use **I wish + Past Simple** to change the present, **I wish + Past Perfect** to undo the past, and **I wish + would** to stop annoying habits!"

> "Never say 'I wish the plane wouldn't crash' for real future hopes—use **I hope** when something is actually possible in the future!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Present Wish): `I wish / If only + Subject + Past Simple`  
Formula (Ongoing Action Wish): `I wish / If only + Subject + Past Continuous`  
Formula (Past Regret): `I wish / If only + Subject + Past Perfect (had / hadn't + V3)`  
Formula (Annoyance / Desired Change in Others): `I wish + Subject + would / wouldn't + Bare Infinitive`  

* **Бажання щодо теперішнього або майбутнього (Wishes about the Present):**  
  Конструкція *I wish / If only + Past Simple* використовується для вираження жалю про поточну ситуацію, яку ми хочемо змінити, але не можемо. Для дієслова *to be* в офіційному мовленні вживається *were* для всіх осіб (*I wish I were/was on a beach*).
* **Бажання щодо триваючої дії у теперішньому (Wishes about Ongoing Actions):**  
  Конструкція *I wish / If only + Past Continuous* описує бажання виконувати іншу дію просто зараз (*I wish I was traveling now*).
* **Жаль про минуле (Wishes / Regrets about the Past):**  
  Конструкція *I wish / If only + Past Perfect* вживається для вираження жалю про події чи вчинки у минулому, які вже неможливо змінити (*I wish I had studied harder*).
* **Роздратування або бажання змінити поведінку інших (Annoyance / Impatience):**  
  Конструкція *I wish + Subject + would / wouldn't + V* використовується, коли нас дратує чиясь поведінка або подія у теперішньому, і ми хочемо, щоб це припинилося (*I wish he would stop smoking*).
* **Відмінність між I wish та I hope:**  
  *I hope* використовується для реальних очікувань у майбутньому (*I hope it's sunny tomorrow*). *I wish* описує уявні, нереальні бажання або жаль.

*Example: I wish I knew Sue's phone number, but I don't.*  
*Example: I wish I was lying on a beach right now instead of sitting in the office.*  
*Example: I wish I hadn't eaten so much food at dinner.*  
*Example: I wish my neighbor would stop playing the piano at night!*  
*Example: I hope the plane arrives on time tomorrow.*  

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Complete the Present Wishes (I wish + Past Simple / Past Continuous)

1. I don't speak French fluently. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish I **spoke** French fluently.
2. She is sitting in the office, but she wants to be on vacation. \\(\rightarrow\\) She wishes she **was/were** on vacation right now.
3. I don't have an umbrella and it's raining. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish I **had** an umbrella.
4. I am working right now, but I want to sleep. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish I **was/were sleeping**.
5. He doesn't know how to drive a car. \\(\rightarrow\\) He wishes he **could drive** a car.

### Exercise 2: Complete the Past Regrets (I wish + Past Perfect)

1. You forgot your umbrella and got wet in the rain. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish I **had taken** my umbrella.
2. He failed his exam because he didn't prepare. \\(\rightarrow\\) He wishes he **had studied** harder for his exam.
3. She didn't bring her camera on the trip. \\(\rightarrow\\) She wishes she **had brought** her camera.
4. I lost my wallet on the street yesterday. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish I **hadn't lost** my wallet.
5. We didn't know about the change in plans earlier. \\(\rightarrow\\) We wish we **had known** about the change in plans earlier.

### Exercise 3: Express Annoyance (I wish + would / wouldn't)

1. My neighbor plays loud music at night and it irritates me. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish he **wouldn't play** the piano at night.
2. My colleague keeps complaining about everything. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish he **wouldn't complain** so much.
3. It's raining heavily and ruining my outdoor plans. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish it **would stop** raining.
4. People drop trash on the streets all the time. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish people **wouldn't litter** on the streets.
5. You are making too much noise while I try to study. \\(\rightarrow\\) I wish you **would stop** making so much noise.

### Exercise 4: True or False Concept Check

1. "I wish I have more time" is grammatically correct. \\(\rightarrow\\) **False** (It should be "I wish I **had** more time").
2. "I wish the plane wouldn't crash tomorrow" is the correct way to express a real hope. \\(\rightarrow\\) **False** (We should say "I **hope** the plane doesn't crash tomorrow").
3. "She wishes she could travel around the world" expresses a present wish. \\(\rightarrow\\) **True**
4. "I wish I hadn't said that" refers to a regret about a past action. \\(\rightarrow\\) **True**

### Translation Block

* Я шкодую, що не знаю її номера телефону. — *I wish I **knew** her phone number.*
* Якби ж я вчився краще минулого тижня! — *I wish I **had studied** harder last week.*
* Я хотів би, щоб мій сусід перестав шуміти. — *I wish my neighbor **would stop** making noise.*
* Вона шкодує, що не взяла фотоапарат у поїздку. — *She wishes she **had brought** her camera on the trip.*
* Я сподіваюся, що завтра буде сонячно. — *I **hope** it will be sunny tomorrow.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What is something you don't know that you wish you knew?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I wish I spoke two or three foreign languages fluently, as it would greatly expand my international professional network.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I wish I knew more about financial investment strategies, though I haven't found the time to study them yet.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't spend much time thinking about things I don't know, to be honest, as I prefer focusing on my current skills.

**Q: Where do you wish you were right now instead of being at work or studying?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I wish I was lying on a quiet Mediterranean beach right now, soaking up the sun and relaxing completely.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I wish I were having a cozy coffee break with my friends, but I have a few pressing tasks to complete first.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I can't complain about my current location, as I actually enjoy my office environment and current project pace.

**Q: What is a past mistake or decision that you wish you hadn't made?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I wish I had started learning software development earlier in my career, as it would have given me a great head start.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I wish I hadn't spent so much money on gadgets last year, though I still use most of them regularly.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't dwell on past regrets very often, to be honest, because every mistake provides a valuable learning experience.

**Q: What is an annoying habit in others that you wish people would stop doing?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I really wish people wouldn't interrupt each other during team meetings, as active listening makes discussions far more productive.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I wish my neighbors wouldn't play loud music late at night, but I usually manage to ignore it.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid annoying habits rarely bother me for long, as I try to maintain a patient and flexible attitude.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Regretting a Missed Deadline & Resource Allocation**
* **Context:** A project manager and a lead developer reflecting on a delayed client release.
* **Role A:** "Could we review why our team struggled to deliver the software module yesterday?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I wish we **had allocated** more testing time last week. We ran into unexpected system integration bugs."
* **Role A:** "I understand. Do you think we should request a deadline extension from the client?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. I wish our department **had communicated** the technical constraints sooner, but we can send a revised schedule now."

**Scenario 2: Addressing Workplace Distractions**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing office noise and focus during a busy workday.
* **Role A:** "How are you coming along with the financial report? You look a bit distracted."
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I wish colleagues in the hallway **would stop making** so much noise while I'm trying to concentrate on complex figures."
* **Role A:** "I completely agree. Would it be helpful if we reserved a quiet meeting room for the afternoon?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, that would be wonderful! I wish I **had thought** of booking a room earlier."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share positive wishes, suggest constructive alternatives, or correct assumptions politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly expressing personal regrets, acknowledging lack of skills, or admitting workplace frustration. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to soften negative statements, state lack of regrets gently, or decline complaints courteously. |
| **I wish we had... earlier** | A polite, constructive formula for analyzing past project oversights in team debriefs without assigning personal blame. |
| **I wish people wouldn't...** | A soft, non-confrontational way to express frustration with annoying habits or office distractions. |
| **I hope everything goes well** | Used to express positive, real expectations for future outcomes in professional correspondence. |
