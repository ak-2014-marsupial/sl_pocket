# 10. What's the Best Travel Style?

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Go backpacking** | To travel carrying everything you need in a backpack, often walking or using public transport | Подорожувати / піти в похід з рюкзаком |
| **Go on a solo trip** | To travel somewhere alone, without other people | Поїхати у соло-подорож (один) |
| **Go on a road trip** | To travel by car or motorbike, usually stopping in different places along the way | Подорожувати автомобілем / автоподорож |
| **Rent a car/bike/scooter** | To pay to use a car, bike, or scooter for a short period of time | Орендувати автомобіль/велосипед/скутер |
| **Have insurance on a rented car** | To pay for a service that gives you money if something bad happens to the rented car | Мати страховку на орендований автомобіль |
| **Narrow streets** | Streets that are small or thin, so it’s hard for cars to pass | Вузькі вулиці |
| **Have a backup plan** | To have another plan ready in case something goes wrong during the journey | Мати план на випадок непередбачених обставин |
| **Book an Airbnb** | To arrange and pay to stay in someone’s house or flat using a website or app | Забронювати квартиру/жило на Airbnb |
| **A well-located hotel** | A hotel that is in a good place, close to key attractions or transport links | Готель зі зручним розташуванням |
| **Book in advance** | To arrange and pay for accommodation or tickets before your trip, not at the last minute | Забронювати заздалегідь |
| **Double check reviews on** | To look at what other people said about a place one more time to be sure of its quality | Подвійно перевірити відгуки про |
| **Couchsurfing** | To stay for free in the home of a local person, often sleeping on their sofa | Кучсерфінг (безкоштовне проживання у місцевих) |
| **Pay the extra cost** | To give more money for something special, extra service, or higher quality | Платити додаткову вартість / доплачувати |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Always **book in advance** and **double check reviews**, but don't forget to **have a backup plan**!"

> "Whether you **go backpacking** or stay in a **well-located hotel**, always make sure to **have insurance on a rented car**!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Preferences): `Subject + prefer + to + Verb (Infinitive) / Verb+ing + rather than + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula (Conditional Planning): `If + Subject + Present Simple, Subject + should / will + Verb (Infinitive)`
Formula (Evaluation): `Subject + sound(s) + Adjective (better / more convenient) + to + Subject`

* **Вираження вподобань у подорожах (Expressing Travel Preferences):**
  Для порівняння різних стилів відпочинку, транспорту чи житла використовуються конструкції *prefer to... rather than...*, *would rather... than...* або фраза *sounds better / more convenient*.
* **Умовне планування та підстраховка (Conditional & Backup Planning):**
  Для ситуацій форс-мажору чи непередбачуваних змін під час поїздки вживається Перший умовний спосіб (*First Conditional*) або модальне дієслово *should* для надання порад.

*Example: I prefer to go on a road trip rather than take a tourist bus.*
*Example: Staying in a well-located hotel sounds much better than couchsurfing if you value comfort.*
*Example: If your flight gets cancelled, you should always have a backup plan.*
*Example: You should book in advance if you plan to travel during the high season.*

## 4. Written Practice

### Exercise 1: Fill in the Gaps (Travel Styles & Transport)

1. Last summer, my friends and I decided to go **backpacking** across Europe with only our backpacks.
2. If you don’t want to depend on buses, you can **rent** a car/bike/scooter when you arrive in Italy.
3. I prefer to go on a **road** trip because I enjoy driving through different towns and stopping whenever I want.
4. It’s always smart to book in **advance** your hotel or tickets, especially in high season.
5. The hotel we stayed in was a **well-located** hotel, just five minutes from the beach.
6. He decided to go on a **solo** trip for the first time, because he wanted to travel independently.

### Exercise 2: Fill in the Gaps (Booking & Safety)

1. When renting a car abroad, it’s very important to have **insurance** on a rented car.
2. In many old towns you will find **narrow** streets where cars can hardly drive.
3. Always double **check** reviews on websites before you make a booking.
4. She chose to try **couchsurfing** instead of staying in a hotel, because she wanted to meet local people.
5. The apartment was cheap, but we had to pay the **extra** cost for cleaning.
6. If your flight gets cancelled, it’s good to have a **backup** plan.

### Exercise 3: Pronunciation & Fluency Practice

1. We decided to **go on a road trip**, **rent a car**, and **have insurance on the rented car** because we planned to drive through **narrow streets**.
2. I wanted to **book an Airbnb**, but in the end, we chose a **well-located hotel** because it was more convenient for sightseeing.
3. It’s always a good idea to **book in advance**, **double check reviews on** the place, and **have a backup plan** if something goes wrong.
4. We tried **couchsurfing** on our trip — it was free and very comfortable, and we didn’t even have to **pay the extra cost** for breakfast!

### Translation Block

* Ми вирішили поїхати в автоподорож, орендувати автомобіль та мати страховку на орендоване авто. — *We decided to **go on a road trip**, **rent a car**, and **have insurance on the rented car**.*
* Завжди розумно забронювати заздалегідь та подвійно перевірити відгуки про це місце. — *It’s always smart to **book in advance** and **double check reviews on** the place.*
* У багатьох старих містах ви знайдете вузькі вулиці, де автомобілі ледве можуть проїхати. — *In many old towns you will find **narrow streets** where cars can hardly drive.*
* Вона вибрала кучсерфінг замість готелю, тому що хотіла познайомитися з місцевими жителями. — *She chose to try **couchsurfing** instead of staying in a hotel, because she wanted to meet local people.*
* Якщо ваш рейс скасують, добре мати план на випадок непередбачених обставин. — *If your flight gets cancelled, it’s good to have a **backup plan**.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: Would you like to go backpacking, go on a solo trip, or go on a road trip one day? Why/why not?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I’d love to go on a road trip along the coast because it gives you total flexibility to explore scenic places at your own pace.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, going on a solo trip sounds interesting, but I think I would prefer a brief road trip with close friends instead.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I wouldn't enjoy going backpacking, as carrying a heavy bag and staying in hostels feels somewhat exhausting to me.

**Q: Do you prefer to rent a car when you travel, or use public transport?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I prefer to rent a car when traveling because it allows us to visit remote spots without relying on bus schedules.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on the destination; in historic European cities with narrow streets, using public transport is much easier.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I avoid renting a car abroad because driving in unfamiliar areas with heavy traffic makes me feel rather anxious.

**Q: Which sounds better to you: couchsurfing or staying in a well-located hotel? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, staying in a well-located hotel sounds significantly better to me because comfort, privacy, and central location are essential.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, couchsurfing could be an intriguing experience for meeting locals, though I usually choose a hotel for security.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid couchsurfing doesn't sound appealing to me at all, to be honest, as I prefer having my own private room and bathroom.

**Q: What would you do if your trip was cancelled? Do you usually have a backup plan?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always make it a rule to have a detailed backup plan and buy flexible tickets so I can reschedule smoothly.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, if my trip got cancelled, I would probably just book a local spa hotel or visit a nearby city for the weekend.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I rarely prepare a backup plan in advance, so a sudden cancellation would leave me feeling quite frustrated.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Planning an International Business Trip**
* **Context:** A corporate travel manager and a senior executive organizing logistics for a conference abroad.
* **Role A:** "Could we finalize the travel arrangements for the upcoming industry conference in Munich?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I'd suggest booking a **well-located hotel** near the exhibition center so we don't waste time in traffic."
* **Role A:** "That makes sense. Should we also **rent a car** at the airport or rely on local public transit?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, given the **narrow streets** in the city center, public transport might be faster, but if we do rent a car, we must **have insurance on a rented car**."

**Scenario 2: Accommodation Booking & Review Verification**
* **Context:** Two colleagues discussing accommodation options for a team retreat.
* **Role A:** "Did you have a chance to look at the apartment options I sent for our team workshop?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. I checked the listing, but I'd recommend we **double check reviews on** other platforms to make sure there are no hidden issues."
* **Role A:** "Good point. What happens if the host cancels our reservation at the last minute?"
* **Role B:** "I'm afraid that could be problematic, so it is essential to **book in advance** and **have a backup plan** ready."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used when sharing positive travel preferences, suggesting comfortable alternatives, or adding details politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when expressing honest views about budget, candid comfort requirements, or practical travel limits gracefully. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion negative responses regarding uncomfortable travel styles, unexpected delays, or missing backup plans. |
| **I'd suggest that we...** | A polite, professional formula for proposing hotel bookings, route adjustments, or transport options to colleagues. |
| **It sounds much better to...** | Used to compare travel choices or accommodation styles in a courteous, non-aggressive manner. |
| **It is essential to...** | A professional, respectful way to highlight important travel precautions like insurance or double-checking reviews. |
