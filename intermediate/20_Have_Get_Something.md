# 20. Have / Get Something Done

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Have / Get something done** | To arrange for a professional or service provider to perform a task for you | Організувати / замовити виконання послуги (коли роблять для вас) |
| **Have someone do something** | To request or hire a specific person to perform an action for you (using bare infinitive) | Попросити / найняти когось зробити щось для вас (без частки to) |
| **Get someone to do something** | To persuade or hire a specific person to perform an action for you (using to-infinitive) | Переконати / найняти когось зробити щось для вас (з часткою to) |
| **Have your hair cut** | To visit a barber or hairdresser to shorten or style your hair | Підстригти волосся у перукаря |
| **Have a car serviced / repaired** | To take a vehicle to a mechanic for routine maintenance or repairs | Провести техогляд / відремонтувати авто на СТО |
| **Have the house cleaned** | To hire professional cleaners to sanitize and tidy your residence | Замовити прибирання будинку |
| **Have teeth checked** | To visit a dentist for a professional oral health examination | Перевірити зуби у стоматолога |
| **Have a website designed** | To employ a web developer or designer to create a site for a company | Замовити розробку вебсайту |
| **Have the kitchen redecorated** | To hire interior specialists to renovate or repaint a kitchen space | Оновити ремонт / інтер'єр кухні (за допомогою фахівців) |
| **Have photos taken** | To hire a professional photographer to take pictures during an event | Сфотографуватися у фотографа / зробити фотосесію |
| **Have shoes repaired** | To bring damaged footwear to a cobbler or repair shop for fixing | Віддати взуття в ремонт |
| **Have the roof repaired** | To hire construction workers or roofers to fix damages on top of a building | Відремонтувати дах (за допомогою робітників) |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "If you **have or get something done**, you arrange for someone else to do a job for you — you don't do it yourself!"

> "Remember the active causative formula: **have someone DO something** (without 'to') vs. **get someone TO DO something** (with 'to')!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Passive Causative — Service by Others): `Subject + have / get + Object (something) + Verb3 / Past Participle`  
Formula (Active Causative with Have): `Subject + have + Person (someone) + Bare Infinitive (do) + Object`  
Formula (Active Causative with Get): `Subject + get + Person (someone) + to + Infinitive (to do) + Object`  

* **Пасивна каузативна конструкція (Passive Causative):**  
  Конструкція `have / get + something + V3` використовується, коли ми організовуємо виконання дії для себе, але її здійснює інша особа (фахівець, майстер, майстриня):  
  * *Present Simple:* *She **gets her nails done** every month.*  
  * *Present Continuous:* *I am **having my phone fixed** right now.*  
  * *Past Simple:* *We **had the house painted** last summer.*  
  * *Future Simple:* *I will **have my hair cut** tomorrow.*  
  * *Modals:* *You should **have your car serviced** before the trip.*

* **Активна каузативна конструкція (Active Causative):**  
  Використовується, коли ми зазначаємо конкретного виконавця послуги. Зверніть увагу на граматичну форму після дієслова:  
  * **Have someone DO something:** після *have* вживається інфінітив **без частки to** (*I had a plumber fix the shower*).  
  * **Get someone TO DO something:** після *get* вживається інфінітив **з часткою to** (*I got a mechanic to check the brakes*).

*Example: She gets her nails done every month at a salon.*  
*Example: I am having my phone fixed right now at the service center.*  
*Example: I had a plumber fix the shower yesterday.*  
*Example: I got the taxi driver to collect us from the airport.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: How often do you have your hair cut or get your nails done at a professional salon?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I make sure to have my hair cut every six weeks and get my nails done twice a month to maintain a neat professional look.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my schedule; I usually have my hair cut before major events or business trips.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't visit salons very often, to be honest, as I prefer simple grooming routines at home.

**Q: Do you prefer fixing household issues yourself, or do you always get a specialist to repair things?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I always get a certified specialist to repair complex appliances or plumbing to ensure safety and quality work.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I try to fix minor issues myself using tutorials, but I get a mechanic or electrician to handle major technical problems.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I'm quite hesitant to attempt DIY repairs, so I always have professionals fix any household breakdown.

**Q: Have you ever had a car serviced or a device repaired where the result didn't meet your expectations?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I had my laptop repaired at an authorized service center recently, and they fixed the mother board flawlessly.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I once had my car serviced and had to return it the next day because the diagnostic sensor was still flagging an error.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I had a bad experience last year when I had my phone screen replaced by an unverified repair shop, as the display color quality dropped.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Arranging Office Renovation & IT Maintenance**
* **Context:** An office manager and an IT lead planning annual facility maintenance.
* **Role A:** "Could we review our quarterly maintenance checklist for the main office floor?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! We're planning to **have the main conference room redecorated** next weekend."
* **Role A:** "Great. Will that affect our server hardware installation?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, no. While the painters work, I will **get an IT technician to service** our network routers and backup systems."

**Scenario 2: Scheduling Vehicle Maintenance Before a Business Trip**
* **Context:** A senior consultant consulting an administrative coordinator before a regional corporate drive.
* **Role A:** "I have an extensive client trip scheduled next week. Is the company car ready for long distances?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, I **had the mechanic check** the engine and **get the brakes fixed** yesterday."
* **Role A:** "Excellent! Did you also manage to clean the exterior?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, not yet, but I will **have the car washed** at the auto detailing center before Monday morning."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to clarify who performed a service, share positive maintenance outcomes, or state facts politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when candidly explaining repair costs, admitting lack of DIY skills, or giving realistic feedback. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion news about delayed repair services, unexpected mechanic costs, or service issues. |
| **We are having... done** | A polite, professional way to inform team members or clients about ongoing maintenance or IT updates. |
| **I'd suggest getting a specialist to...** | A polite, non-confrontational formula for advising colleagues to hire professional services instead of DIY. |
