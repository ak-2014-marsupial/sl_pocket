# 39. From Hobby to Profession

## 1. Vocabulary & Definitions

| Word/Phrase | Definition in English | Переклад |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Take self-defense classes** | To attend lessons where you learn physical techniques to protect yourself from danger | Проходити курси самозахисту / відвідувати заняття із самооборони |
| **Play a cello / sax / drums** | To create music using a cello, saxophone, or drum set | Грати на віолончелі / саксофоні / барабанах |
| **Attend escape rooms** | To participate in an interactive game where you solve puzzles to exit a locked room | Відвідувати квест-кімнати |
| **Breed animals** | To keep, care for, and raise animals so that they produce offspring | Розводити тварин |
| **Do pottery** | To craft ceramic items such as bowls, cups, or plates from clay using your hands | Займатися гончарством |
| **Do calligraphy** | To write letters and text in a decorative, elegant, and artistic style | Займатися каліграфією |
| **Be into** | To be extremely interested in, enthusiastic about, or fond of a particular activity | Захоплюватися чимось |
| **Be keen on** | To like or be very interested in something | Дуже подобатися / захоплюватися |
| **Can't get enough of** | To enjoy something so much that you want to do or experience it constantly | Обожнювати / не могти натішитися чимось |
| **Totally my thing** | A phrase used to express that an activity or subject perfectly suits your personality or interests | Це точно моє / прямо по мені |
| **Depend on your mood** | To be determined by or change according to how one feels at a specific time | Залежати від настрою |
| **Not see the point of** | To fail to understand the reason, value, or benefit of doing something | Не бачити сенсу у чомусь |

## 2. Vocabulary Hooks

> "Find what is **totally your thing**—whether you **do pottery** or **take self-defense classes**—and do it until you **can't get enough of** it!"

> "When choosing a new passion, remember that it might **depend on your mood**, but you should never spend time on activities where you **don't see the point of** them!"

## 3. Grammar Essentials

Formula (Interest + Noun): `Subject + be into / be keen on / can't get enough of + Noun`  
Formula (Interest + Gerund): `Subject + be into / be keen on / can't get enough of + Verb-ing`  
Formula (Suitability): `Activity (Noun / Gerund) + is / isn't + (totally) my thing`  
Formula (Evaluation): `Subject + don't / doesn't see the point of + Verb-ing / Noun`  

* **Вираження захоплень та зацікавленості (Expressing Hobbies & Preferences):**  
  Для опису своїх хобі, пристрастей та інтересів вживаються дієслівні фрази з прийменниками (*be into, be keen on, can't get enough of*). Після прийменників завжди вживається **іменник** або **герундій (Verb-ing)**.
* **Сленгові та розмовні вирази прихильності (Informal Preference Expressions):**  
  Фраза *totally my thing* використовується для підкреслення того, що діяльність ідеально підходить людині за характером та вподобаннями.
* **Вираження скепсису або залежності від стану (Skeptical & Variable Preferences):**  
  * *Depend on one's mood* показує мінливість уподобань залежно від емоційного стану.  
  * *Not see the point of + Verb-ing* виражає відсутність розуміння доцільності чи користі від певної діяльності.

*Example: I am really into pottery these days.*  
*Example: She is keen on doing calligraphy in her free time.*  
*Example: They can't get enough of escape rooms.*  
*Example: Self-defense is totally my thing.*  
*Example: For me, choosing a weekend activity depends on my mood.*  
*Example: I don't see the point of watching sports on TV.*

### Translation Block

* Вона дуже захоплюється каліграфією у вільний час. — *She **is keen on** doing calligraphy in her free time.*
* Вони обожнюють квест-кімнати та відвідують їх щовихідних. — *They **can't get enough of** escape rooms and visit them every weekend.*
* Гончарство — це точно моє, я можу займатися цим годинами. — *Pottery **is totally my thing**; I could do it for hours.*
* Вибір фільму для мене залежить від настрою. — *For me, choosing a movie **depends on my mood**.*
* Я не бачу сенсу у розведенні тварин, якщо у вас немає великого подвір'я. — *I **don't see the point of** breeding animals unless you have a large yard.*

## 5. Speaking & Discussion Guide

**Q: What creative or active hobbies are you really into these days?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I'm really into doing pottery these days because crafting ceramic cups with my hands helps me unwind after long office hours.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, it depends on my mood; I occasionally do calligraphy on weekends, but I don't practice it daily.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I haven't found a creative hobby that feels totally my thing yet, to be honest.

**Q: Have you ever considered turning a personal hobby into a full-time profession?**
* **Positive:** Actually, I would love to turn my passion for photography into a profession because I can't get enough of capturing authentic moments.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, I enjoy keeping my hobbies separate from my career, as monetizing a passion can sometimes make it feel like routine work.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I don't see the point of making my hobby a profession, to be honest, because I value having a completely pressure-free activity.

**Q: What is an activity that you don't see the point of? Why?**
* **Positive:** Actually, while I respect other people's interests, I don't quite see the point of playing computer games for hours when you could be outdoors.
* **Neutral:** To be honest, attending escape rooms isn't really my thing, though I understand why people who love puzzles enjoy them.
* **Negative:** I'm afraid I find extreme sports far too risky, so I don't see the point of taking unnecessary physical chances.

## 6. Professional Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Monetizing a Creative Passion & Career Transition**
* **Context:** A career advisor and a client discussing transitioning from a corporate role to a creative small business.
* **Role A:** "Could we discuss your plans for transitioning into a new professional field this year?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes. I've been **keen on doing pottery** for three years, and I'm considering launching my own ceramic studio."
* **Role A:** "That sounds like an exciting venture! Do you feel confident about turning your passion into a stable income?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, I know managing a business will be intense, but crafting handmade products is **totally my thing**, and clients already love my designs."

**Scenario 2: Team Icebreaker & Sharing Personal Interests**
* **Context:** Two colleagues exchanging personal interests during an informal team lunch.
* **Role A:** "How do you usually spend your time outside work? Do you have any active hobbies?"
* **Role B:** "To be honest, **it depends on my mood**. On weekdays I usually relax, but on Saturdays, I **take self-defense classes**."
* **Role A:** "That's impressive! Do you enjoy the physical training?"
* **Role B:** "Actually, yes! I **can't get enough of** learning new defensive moves — it gives me a great sense of confidence."

## 7. Polite English Cheat Sheet

| Phrase / Softener | When to use it (Context) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actually** | Used to share enthusiasm for hobbies, announce career transitions, or clarify personal choices politely. |
| **To be honest** | Used when admitting variable interest in activities, sharing candid views on monetizing hobbies, or expressing neutral preferences. |
| **I'm afraid (that)** | Used to cushion statements when declining interest in certain hobbies or expressing polite skepticism. |
| **It is totally my thing** | A soft, natural expression used to communicate strong personal alignment with an activity or career path. |
| **It depends on my mood** | Used in casual and professional icebreakers to explain flexible leisure preferences gracefully. |
| **I don't quite see the point of...** | A polite, non-confrontational way to state that a particular activity doesn't appeal to you personally. |
